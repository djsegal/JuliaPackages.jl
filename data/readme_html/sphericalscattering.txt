<p dir="auto">This package provides semi-analytical solutions to the scattering of time harmonic and static electromagnetic fields from spherical objects (amongst others known as Mie solutions or Mie scattering).
To this end, series expansions are evaluated. Special care is taken to obtain accurate solutions down to the static limit.</p>
<p dir="auto">The following aspects are implemented (✔) and planned (⌛):</p>
