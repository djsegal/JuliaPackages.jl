<p dir="auto">KinematicCoordinateTransformations.jl is a Julia package for performing chains of coordinate system transformations involving not only position, but also kinematic quantities such as velocity, acceleration, and jerk.
The transformations currently supported:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>ConstantAffineMap</code>: transformation of the form <code>x_new = A*x + b</code> for input vector <code>x</code>, where <code>A</code> and <code>b</code> are independent of time.</li>
<li><code>ConstantLinearMap</code>: transformation of the form <code>x_new = A*x</code> for input vector <code>x</code>, where <code>A</code> is independent of time.</li>
<li><code>ConstantVelocityTransformation</code>: transformation of the form <code>x_new = x .+ x0 .+ (t - t0).*v0</code>, where <code>x0</code>, <code>t0</code>, and <code>v0</code> are parameters associated with the transformation, and <code>t</code> is time.</li>
<li><code>SteadyRot{X,Y,Z}Transformation</code>: steady rotations about the <code>X</code>, <code>Y</code> or <code>Z</code> axes. "Steady" here means rotating at a constant rate.</li>
</ul>
