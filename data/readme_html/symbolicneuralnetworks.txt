<p dir="auto">SymbolicNeuralNetworks.jl was created to take advantage of <a href="https://symbolics.juliasymbolics.org/stable/" rel="nofollow">Symbolics.jl</a> for training neural networks by accelarating their evaluation and by simplifing the computation of some derivatives of the neural network that may be needed for loss functions. This package is based on <a href="https://github.com/JuliaGNI/AbstractNeuralNetworks.jl">AbstractNeuralNetwork.jl</a> and can be applied to <a href="https://github.com/JuliaGNI/GeometricMachineLearning.jl">GeometricMachineLearning.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">To accelerate the evaluation of the neural network, we change its evaluation method with its code generated by <a href="https://symbolics.juliasymbolics.org/stable/" rel="nofollow">Symbolics.jl</a>, performs some otpmizations on it, and generate the associate function with <a href="https://github.com/SciML/RuntimeGeneratedFunctions.jl">RuntimeGeneratedFunctions.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">One can easily symbolize its neural network which will create another neural networks with the symbolize method</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="symbolize(neuralnet, dim)"><pre><span class="pl-c1">symbolize</span>(neuralnet, dim)</pre></div>
<p dir="auto">where neuralnet is a neural network in the framework of  <a href="https://github.com/JuliaGNI/AbstractNeuralNetworks.jl">AbstractNeuralNetwork.jl</a> and dim the dimension of the input.</p>
