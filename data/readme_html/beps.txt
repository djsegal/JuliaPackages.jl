<blockquote>
<p dir="auto">Dongdong Kong</p>
<p dir="auto">BEPS.jl is alive in Julia now. All functions have been ported to Julia, and the
performance is about 2.5 times faster than C version.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Julia: 0.286327 seconds (822.38 k allocations: 22.998 MiB, 0.85% gc time)</li>
<li>C    : 0.787059 seconds (629.95 k allocations: 13.915 MiB)</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
