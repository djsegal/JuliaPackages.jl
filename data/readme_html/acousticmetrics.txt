<p dir="auto">AcousticMetrics.jl is a Julia package for computing various metrics useful in acoustics.
Currently implemented metrics include:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto">Various narrowband spectra</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Pressure amplitude</li>
<li>Mean-squared pressure amplitude (MSP)</li>
<li>Power Spectral Density (PSD)</li>
<li>Phase</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Proportional band spectra</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Approximate octave and third-octave spectra</li>
<li>Exact proportional spectra of any octave fraction &gt; 0.</li>
<li>Lazy representations of proportional band spectra constructed from either other narrowband or proportional band spectra</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Integrated metrics</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Unweighted and A-weighted Overall Sound Pressure Level (OASPL)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
