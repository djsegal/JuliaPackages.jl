<p dir="auto">For a brief introduction to ZXCalculus, please check <a href="https://github.com/ChenZhao44/ZXCalculusTutorials">this notebook</a> or <a href="https://chenzhao44.github.io/2020/08/27/ZXCalculus.jl/" rel="nofollow">this blog post</a>.</p>
