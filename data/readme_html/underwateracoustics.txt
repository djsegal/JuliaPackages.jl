<ul dir="auto">
<li>Install <a href="https://github.com/org-arl/AcousticRayTracers.jl"><code>AcousticRayTracers.jl</code></a> for <code>RaySolver</code> model</li>
<li>Install <a href="https://github.com/org-arl/AcousticsToolbox.jl"><code>AcousticsToolbox.jl</code></a> for <code>Bellhop</code> and <code>Kraken</code> models</li>
<li>Install <a href="https://github.com/org-arl/DataDrivenAcoustics.jl"><code>DataDrivenAcoustics.jl</code></a> for <code>RayBasis</code> and <code>GPR</code> family of models</li>
</ul>
