<p dir="auto">Load astronomical images of the sky and process them with convenient, general, and composable functions.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>SkyImages.jl</code> focuses on the functionality to load images, access their data, and optionally transform them to the shape most suitable for further analysis</p>
<p dir="auto">Loaded images are directly <strong>represented as keyed arrays</strong> (from <code>AxisKeys.jl</code>) with axis keys being special objects defined here in <code>SkyImages.jl</code>. These axis key objects know how to translate between pixels and sky coordinates on the basis of FITS WCS or other projection (see e.g. a healpix example below).</p>
<p dir="auto">It's most convenient to work with regular rectangular array, and <code>SkyImages</code> gives multiple way to obtain image in such form. It can <strong>project onto any reasonable coordinate grid</strong>, or <strong>extract the original data array</strong> as stored in FITS with its coordinates.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>SkyImages</code> provides plotting integration with <code>Makie</code> for ease of use and exploration.</p>
<p dir="auto">See the <a href="https://aplavin.github.io/SkyImages.jl/test/notebook.html" rel="nofollow">Pluto notebook</a> for more details and examples. <em>The older notebook with PyPlot examples remains available <a href="https://aplavin.github.io/SkyImages.jl/test/notebook.jl.html" rel="nofollow">here</a>.</em></p>
