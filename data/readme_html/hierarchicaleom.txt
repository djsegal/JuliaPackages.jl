<p dir="auto"><code>HierarchicalEOM.jl</code> is a numerical framework written in <a href="https://julialang.org/" rel="nofollow"><code>Julia</code></a>. It provides a user-friendly and efficient tool based on hierarchical equations of motion (HEOM) approach to simulate complex open quantum systems, including non-Markovian effects due to non-perturbative interaction with one (or multiple) environment(s). It is built upon <a href="https://github.com/qutip/QuantumToolbox.jl"><code>QuantumToolbox.jl</code></a>.</p>
