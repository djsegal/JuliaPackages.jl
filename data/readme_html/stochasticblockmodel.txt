<p dir="auto">StochasticBlockModel.jl is a Julia package that provides methods for common tasks related to Stochastic Block Models (SBM), such as generating random graphs with community structure and detecting communities in a given network. Community detection is performed by estimating the parameters of a SBM from an observed graph. Different heuristics and one exact method are provided for the problem of finding the maximum log-likelihood estimate of the parameters of a SBM.</p>
