<p dir="auto"><code>RitSpls</code> implements a robustification of Sparse Partial Least Squares (SPLS) through robustness-inducing transformations on the (univariate) predictand and predictors.</p>
<p dir="auto">The approach considered in [1] is:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto">transform multivariate predictors through a Generalized Spatial Sign Pre-Processing (GSSPP) [2];</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">transform the univariate predictand through a Wrapping transformation [3];</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">use the SNIPLS (sparse NIPALS) algorithm [4] to estimate an SPLS model from the (robustly) transformed inputs.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p dir="auto">The package provides an <code>SPLS</code> class that allows to interface with the ScikitLearn API.
For instance, objects of the <code>SPLS</code> and <code>GSSPP</code> class accept widely used ScikitLearn functions and routines (e.g, <code>fit!</code>, <code>predict</code>, <code>transform</code>, <code>GridSearchCV</code>, etc.).</p>
