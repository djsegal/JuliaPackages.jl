<p dir="auto">Train and sample a <em>standardized</em> Restricted Boltzmann machine in Julia. The energy is given by:</p>
<p dir="auto"><math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="8ec854f2308451fbc9521ebaea876f90">$$E(\mathbf{v},\mathbf{h}) = - \sum_{i}\theta_{i}v_{i} - \sum_{\mu}\theta_{\mu}h_{\mu} - \sum_{i\mu}w_{i\mu} \frac{v_{i} - \lambda_{i}}{\sigma_{i}}\frac{h_{\mu} - \lambda_{\mu}}{\sigma_{\mu}}$$</math-renderer></p>
<p dir="auto">with some offset parameters <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="8ec854f2308451fbc9521ebaea876f90">$\lambda_i,\lambda_\mu$</math-renderer> and scaling parameters <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="8ec854f2308451fbc9521ebaea876f90">$\sigma_i,\sigma_\mu$</math-renderer>. Usually <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="8ec854f2308451fbc9521ebaea876f90">$\lambda_i,\lambda_\mu$</math-renderer> track the mean activities of visible and hidden units, while <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="8ec854f2308451fbc9521ebaea876f90">$\sigma_i,\sigma_\mu$</math-renderer> track their standard deviations.</p>
