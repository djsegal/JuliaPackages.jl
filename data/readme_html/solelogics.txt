<p dir="auto"><em>SoleLogics.jl</em> provides a fresh codebase for computational logic, featuring easy manipulation of:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Propositional and (multi)modal logics (atoms, logical constants, alphabets, grammars, crisp/fuzzy algebras);</li>
<li>Logical formulas (parsing, random generation, minimization);</li>
<li>Logical interpretations (e.g., propositional valuations, Kripke structures);</li>
<li>Algorithms for <em>finite <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_checking" rel="nofollow">model checking</a></em>, that is, checking that a formula is satisfied by an interpretation.</li>
</ul>
