<p dir="auto"><em>MonotoneDecomposition.jl</em> is a Julia package for monotone decomposition with <a href="https://github.com/szcf-weiya/MonotoneSplines.jl">MonotoneSplines.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Check the following paper for more details.</p>
<blockquote>
<p dir="auto">Lijun Wang, Xiaodan Fan, Hongyu Zhao, and Jun S. Liu. “Decomposition with Monotone B-Splines: Fitting and Testing.” arXiv, January 12, 2024. <a href="http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06383" rel="nofollow">http://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06383</a>.</p>
</blockquote>
