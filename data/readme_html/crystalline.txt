<p dir="auto">Tools for crystalline symmetry analysis implemented in the Julia language.</p>
<p dir="auto">This package provides access to the symmetry operations of crystalline point groups, space groups, Wyckoff positions, their irreducible representations and band representations, as well as tools for their associated manipulation.</p>

<p dir="auto">Additional functionality includes e.g. point group operations (<code>pointgroup</code>) and irreps (<code>pgirreps</code>), elementary band representations (<code>bandreps</code>), Wyckoff positions (<code>wyckoffs</code>), conjugacy classes (<code>classes</code>), class-specific characters (<code>classcharacters</code>), group generators (<code>generators</code>), subperiodic groups (<code>subperiodicgroup</code>), 3D magnetic space groups (<code>mspacegroup</code>), and physically real irreps (<code>realify</code>).
In addition, Bravais lattice utilities and conventions are accessible via the lightweight stand-alone sub-package <a href="https://github.com/thchr/Crystalline.jl/tree/master/Bravais">Bravais.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">For a full description of the public API, see the <a href="https://thchr.github.io/Crystalline.jl/dev" rel="nofollow">documentation</a>.</p>
