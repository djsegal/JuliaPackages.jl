<p dir="auto">MicrobeAgents.jl (previously Bactos.jl) is a Julia framework for agent-based
simulations of microbial behavior (especially bacteria), built on
the amazing <a href="https://github.com/JuliaDynamics/Agents.jl">Agents.jl</a>.
MicrobeAgents.jl extends and re-exports a minimal set of Agents.jl
functions and structures to be used as a stand-alone package, but it is
recommended to use it alongside Agents.jl for extra niceties.</p>
<p dir="auto">The package is still at an early stage of intense development.
Contributions, requests and suggestions are more than welcome.</p>

<ul dir="auto">
<li>Multiple swimming strategies (run-tumble, run-reverse, run-reverse-flick) with tunable parameters, and possibility to implement your own with minimal effort</li>
<li>Classical and modern models of chemotaxis (Berg-Purcell, Celani-Vergassola, Xie, Brumley)</li>
<li>Support for arbitrary concentration fields, both numerical and analytical</li>
<li>Compatible with DifferentialEquations.jl for parallel integration of external fields and bacterial behavior</li>
<li>Motility in complex environments through Agents.Pathfinding</li>
<li>Analysis routines for standard quantities of interest (MSD, autocorrelation functions, drift velocity)</li>
</ul>
