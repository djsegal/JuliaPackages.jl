<p dir="auto">This package provides both a range of auditory stimulus generation techniques as well as
reverse correlation reconstruction (RC) functions.</p>
<p dir="auto">The stimulus generation methods are intended to be used in RC experimental paradigms
related to reconstructing the internal representations of tinnitus.
However, the functionality is generic and may be useful in many other scenarios.</p>

<p dir="auto">Generate 500 random stimuli and simulate ideal responses for each.
Then, compute the linear (<code>gs</code>) and compressed sensing (<code>cs</code>) reconstructions
and correlate the reconstruction with the binned target signal to determine reconstruction quality.</p>
