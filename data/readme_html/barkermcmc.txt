<p dir="auto">A Monte Carlo Markov Chain sampler that makes use of gradient
information. Proposed by Livingstone et al. (2021)</p>
<p dir="auto">The adaptative preconditioning is based on Andrieu and Thoms (2008),
Algorithm 4 in Section 5. For details see Algorithm 7.2 of the supporting information
of Livingstone et al. (2021).</p>

<p dir="auto">The sampler can used in two ways:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>defining the log density compatible to <a href="https://github.com/tpapp/LogDensityProblems.jl">LogDensityProblems.jl</a>, or</li>
<li>providing two seperate functions for the log density and it's gradient.</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">See the <a href="http://scheidan.github.io/BarkerMCMC.jl/dev" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> for examples of both approaches.</p>
