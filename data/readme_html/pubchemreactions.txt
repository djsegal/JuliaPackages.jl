<p dir="auto">Let us suppose that you have two components, <strong>Glucose</strong> and <strong>Water</strong>, and you want to find potential BioChemical reactions &amp; details associated with it.</p>
<p dir="auto">We'll just need the name of the species we are interested and it will generate a symbolic variable with appropiate metadata:</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="using PubChemReactions, Catalyst

H2O = PubChemReactions.search_compound(&quot;water&quot;)"><pre><span class="pl-k">using</span> PubChemReactions, Catalyst
