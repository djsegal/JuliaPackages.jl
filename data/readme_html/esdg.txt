<p dir="auto">These codes are inspired by the Matlab codes for the book <a href="https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-72067-8" rel="nofollow">Nodal Discontinuous Galerkin methods</a> by Hesthaven and Warburton (2007).  While unstructured meshes are supported, all demos use uniform meshes, and the codebase is intended mainly for experimentation and method development.</p>
