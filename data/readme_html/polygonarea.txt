<p dir="auto">A minimalistic generic Julia package to compute the area of unions and intersections of polygons.</p>
<p dir="auto">Features:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Pure Julia with almost no dependancies,</li>
<li>Generic types (coordinates can be floats, rational numbers or anything else),</li>
<li>Compatible with ForwardDiff.jl,</li>
<li>Non-convex polygons are supported as union of convex polygons,</li>
<li>Plot the polygons with Plots.jl.</li>
</ul>
