<p dir="auto"><strong>μspel</strong>, or muspel, is a Julia package for radiative transfer in stellar atmospheres. It is named after Muspelheim, the realm of fire in Norse mythology. Muspelheim, or muspel, is a <em>hot and glowing land of fire</em>, in some ways perhaps akin to the glowing atmospheres of stars.</p>
<p dir="auto">μspel is still under development, and the API is subject to change. <a href="https://tiagopereira.github.io/Muspel.jl/dev" rel="nofollow">Documentation</a> is currently limited to that generated from docstrings.</p>
