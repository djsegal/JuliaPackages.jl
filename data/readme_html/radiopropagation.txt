<p dir="auto">Julia package implementing radio propagation models.</p>
<p dir="auto">The following is implemented:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Two-Ray propagation model.</li>
<li>Rain attenuation for frequencies in the range 1-400 GHz.</li>
<li>Fog attenuation for frequencies above 5 GHz.</li>
<li>Atmospheric attenuation for frequencies up to 1000 GHz.</li>
</ul>
