<p dir="auto">Adjusted Normalized Degree of Concordance (ANDC) is a similarity measure between two fuzzy (or hard) clusterings. The value is in <code>(-inf, 1]</code>, with 1 representing identical clusterings, and 0 representing the clusterings have the same agreement as "random" clusterings. The selection of "random" is required by the user, and four models are provided.</p>
