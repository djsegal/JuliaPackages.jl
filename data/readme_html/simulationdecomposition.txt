<p dir="auto"><strong>Simulation decomposition</strong> or <strong>SimDec</strong> is an uncertainty and sensitivity
analysis method, which is based on Monte Carlo simulation. SimDec consists of
three major parts:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>computing significance indices,</li>
<li>creating multi-variable scenarios and mapping the output values to them, and</li>
<li>visualizing the scenarios on the output distribution by color-coding its segments.</li>
</ol>
<p dir="auto">SimDec reveals the nature of causalities and interaction effects in the model.
See our <a href="https://www.simdec.fi/publications" rel="nofollow">publications</a> and join our
<a href="https://discord.gg/54SFcNsZS4" rel="nofollow">discord community</a>.</p>
