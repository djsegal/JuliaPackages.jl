<p dir="auto"><code>Walrus.jl</code> is a Julia package providing various closures models (closure -&gt; seal -&gt; seal 🦭 -&gt; walrus) for ocean flavoured fluid dynamics with <a href="https://github.com/CliMA/Oceananigans.jl/">Oceananigans.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Currently, it includes:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>WallStress</code>: a wall stress model for LES</li>
<li><code>TidalForcing</code>: simple body forcing to replicate tides</li>
<li><code>RadiativeHeating</code>: a (very) simple radiative transfer model that imparts a body heating</li>
<li><code>WindStress</code>: a bulk formulation for wind stress</li>
<li><code>SurfaceHeatExchange</code>: a bulk formulation for heat exchange at the ocean surface</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Details of the parametrisations used for each model and implementation notes can be found in their docstrings and in <a href="https://walrus.jagosw.com/stable/appendix/library/" rel="nofollow">the documentation</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">If you have any questions or suggestions please get in touch through an <a href="https://github.com/jagoosw/Walrus.jl/issues">issue</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">If you use this package in your work please contact me so that I can expedite the process of making it citable.</p>
