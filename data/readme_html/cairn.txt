<p dir="auto">Cairn.jl is a toolkit of active learning algorithms for training machine learning interatomic potentials (ML-IPs) for molecular dynamics simulation.</p>
<p dir="auto">Cairn.jl is constructed as an extension to <a href="https://github.com/JuliaMolSim/Molly.jl">Molly.jl</a>, implementing enhanced MD samplers, and interfaces with other packages in the Julia ecosystem for molecular simulation, developed by <a href="https://github.com/cesmix-mit">CESMIX</a> and <a href="https://github.com/JuliaMolSim">JuliaMolSim</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Active learning algorithms build efficient training datasets which maximally improve accuracy of a scientific machine learning model. These algorithms take an iterative structure, looping through the steps:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Data generation</strong>. A system's potential energy landscape is sampled by generating trajectories of molecular configurations through the simulation of Newton's equation of motion or its modifications. Users have a choice between standard MD simulation, such as Langevin dynamics or Velocity-Verlet, or enhanced sampling algorithms, such as uncertainty driven dynamics (<a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-023-00406-5" rel="nofollow">UDD</a>), Stein repulsive Langevin dynamics, or Stein variational molecular dynamics. These methods are specified under the abstract type <code>Simulator</code>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Trigger for retraining.</strong> Sampling is terminated and retraining is triggered when the trajectory has met a certain criteria. A "fixed trigger" calls on retraining after a fixed number of simulation steps. "Adaptive triggers" are based on metrics of uncertainty, from Gaussian process or ensemble-based estimates of variance; metrics of extrapolation, based on a MaxVol vector basis; or metrics of diversity, such as a DPP inclusion probability. These methods are specified under the abstract type <code>ActiveLearningTrigger</code>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Data subset selection and labelling.</strong> A subset of the data from the simulated trajectory is selected for labelling using reference calculations and appending to the training set. The most basic selection is a random subset of the trajectory. "Adaptive" selections can be made based on data which exceeds a threshold or data which are chosen by a subset selection algorithm, such as MaxVol, k-means clustering, or DPPs. These methods are specified under the abstract type <code>SubsetSelector</code>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Model updating.</strong> The machine learning model is retrained on the augmented dataset according to the choice of objective function defined by the abstract type <code>LinearProblem</code>. These methods live in the package <a href="https://github.com/cesmix-mit/PotentialLearning.jl">PotentialLearning.jl</a>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p dir="auto">For a technical manual on the package, see the <a href="cesmix-mit.github.io/Cairn.jl/">docs</a>. For a demo, see the Jupyter notebooks in the <code>examples</code> folder.</p>
