<p dir="auto">This is a package for generating <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancichinetti-Fortunato-Radicchi_benchmark" rel="nofollow">Lancichinetti-Fortunato-Radicchi
model</a> benchmarks graphs,
which are widely used in the study of community detection algorithms.</p>
