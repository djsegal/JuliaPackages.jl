<h1 dir="auto"></h1>
<p dir="auto">This project implements standard and scattershot BosonSampling in Julia, including boson samplers and certification and optimization tools.</p>

<p dir="auto">A wide variety of tools are available:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Boson-samplers, including partial distinguishability and loss</li>
<li>Bunching tools and functions</li>
<li>Various tools to validate experimental boson-samplers</li>
<li>User-defined optical circuits built from optical elements</li>
<li>Optimization functions over unitary matrices</li>
<li>Photon counting tools for subsets and partitions of the output modes</li>
<li>Tools to study permanent and generalized matrix function conjectures and counter-examples</li>
</ul>

<p dir="auto">To install the package, launch a Julia REPL session and type</p>

</code></pre></div>
<p dir="auto">Alternatively type on the <code>]</code> key. Then enter</p>
<div class="snippet-clipboard-content notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="add BosonSampling"><pre class="notranslate"><code>add BosonSampling
</code></pre></div>
<p dir="auto">To use the package, write</p>
<div class="snippet-clipboard-content notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="using BosonSampling"><pre class="notranslate"><code>using BosonSampling
