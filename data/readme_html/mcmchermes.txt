<p dir="auto">McmcHermes is a pure-Julia implementation of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis%E2%80%93Hastings_algorithm" rel="nofollow">Metropolis Hasting Algorithm</a> under an MIT license. McmcHermes will help you if you want to estimate model parameters or sample a probability density distribution.</p>
