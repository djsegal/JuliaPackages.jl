<p dir="auto">Efficient collections (arrays and dictionaries) with <code>Union</code> element types. Compared to <code>Vector{Union{...}}</code>, more operations are type-stable: most notably, <code>map()</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Under the hood, elements of different types are stored in separate sub-collections. This allows for operations on the collection as a whole to be type-stable, while <code>X[i]</code> fundamentally remain type-unstable.</p>
