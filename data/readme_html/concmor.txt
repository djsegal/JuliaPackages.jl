<p dir="auto">Library for constructing the "Ritz" transformation matrices for the Coherent Node Cluster model-order reduction.</p>
<p dir="auto">Based on the paper accepted for publication as NME-Jul-19-0522.R1 - Rapid Free-vibration analysis with Model Reduction based on Coherent Nodal Clusters, in the International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, 03/03/2020. Authors: Krysl, Petr, and Sivapuram, Raghavendra; both of University of California, San Diego , Jacobs School of Engineering Department of Structural Engineering; and
Abawi, Ahmad, of Heat, Light, and Sound Research, Inc.</p>
<p dir="auto">This package works with <a href="https://github.com/PetrKryslUCSD/FinEtools.jl"><code>FinEtools</code></a>. If you need the model reduction package to work with <a href="https://github.com/PetrKryslUCSD/Elfel.jl"><code>Elfel</code></a>, please use <a href="https://github.com/PetrKryslUCSD/CoNCMOR2.jl"><code>CoNCMOR2</code></a>.</p>
