<p dir="auto">Microstates.jl is a package designed to calculate the probabilities associated with microstates from a Recurrence Plot. Based on these probabilities, it can also estimate the entropy of the microstates for a given time series. These methods are useful in the context of dynamical systems, dealing with chaotic, stochastic, or periodic time series.</p>
