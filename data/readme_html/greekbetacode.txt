<p dir="auto">This package partially implements the greek parts of the Thesaurus Linguae Graecae, TLG®, Beta
Code. The rationale is to deal with greek text only (thus leaving out control and markup beta
codes, as well as latin, coptic, hebrew, and a plethora of seemingly unrelated codes). In
particular, editions of Greek New Testaments with critical apparata, such as SBL's are the
must-cover character set for the library. Beyond this "core" application, the selection of
covered characters has been somewhat arbitrary, mainly focusing on familiar characters in the
U+00XX, U+03XX, and U+20XX Unicode[3] codepoint ranges.</p>
<p dir="auto">The package is fairly straight-forward, providing two conversion functions: (1) From Beta to
Unicode, and (2) From Unicode to Beta. The functions are erspectively named <code>U</code> and <code>B</code>:</p>

<p dir="auto">Some conversion rules are enforced in case of ambiguity, such as:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>Always prefer Unicode characters with diacritics over plain characters folowed by combining
diacritics; This is done by always preferring the lesser number of Unicode codepoints.</li>
<li>Always prefer Unicode characters of lower codepoint values for the same glyph.</li>
<li>In Beta, diacritics always follows, i.e. are given after, the target character.</li>
<li>In Beta, breathings such as psili and dasia, precede accents.</li>
<li>In Beta, accents such as oxia, varia, perispomeni, and dialiticas, precede subscribed iotas.</li>
<li>When generating Beta, dialitica accents are always placed last.</li>
</ol>
