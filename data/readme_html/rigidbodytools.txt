<p dir="auto">The purpose of this package is to provide tools for rigid bodies with
point-discretized surfaces. It currently includes methods for</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>a library of 2D surface shape definitions and associated point discretizations</li>
<li>calculation of geometric properties</li>
<li>collections of multiple rigid bodies</li>
<li>prescribed kinematics of single or linked systems of rigid and deforming bodies, with a library of joints</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">These tools support a variety of classes of problems, including those that involve bodies interacting with fluids, in which we would immerse these point-discretized representations into a computational grid.</p>
