<p dir="auto">YOLOv7.jl provide one constructor that loads pre-trained weights that were made available by the <a href="https://github.com/WongKinYiu/yolov7">original author implementation in PyTorch</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Currently we only support loading the "standard" version of YOLOv7.</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="using YOLOv7, Flux
