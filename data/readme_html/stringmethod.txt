<p dir="auto">Julia implementation of the String Method for computing transition paths and
saddle points on energy landscapes.  This also implements the Climbing Image
Method.</p>
<p dir="auto">This currently only implements the simplified string method as developed in E,
Ren, and Vanden-Eijnden (2007).  These are but two method amongst many for
finding minimum energy pathways (MEP), and saddle point search strategies.  See,
also, the Nudged Elastic Band method (NEB).</p>
