<p dir="auto">A Julia package for efficient simulation, statistical inference and verification of
Markov Processes/Continuous Time Markov Chains (CTMC) modeled by Biochemical networks/Chemical Reaction Networks (CRN)
with Approximate Bayesian Computation methods (ABC).</p>
<p dir="auto">It implements:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>A core for simulation of Markov Processes/CTMC,</li>
<li>A simple interface for Biochemical Networks/Stochastic Petri Nets,</li>
<li>Synchronized simulation with Linear Hybrid Automata.</li>
<li>Approximate Bayesian Computation (a likelihood-free inference method),</li>
<li>Automaton-ABC: a statistical method for verification of parametric CTMC.</li>
</ul>
