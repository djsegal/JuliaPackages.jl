<p dir="auto">MultilevelEstimators is a Julia package for Multilevel Monte Carlo simulations. It contains the following methods:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto">Monte Carlo (MC) and Quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Multilevel Monte Carlo (MLMC) and Multilevel Quasi-Monte Carlo (MLQMC)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Multi-Index Monte Carlo (MIMC) and Multi-Index Quasi-Monte Carlo (MIQMC)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Adaptive Multi-Index Monte Carlo (A-MIMC) and Adaptive Multi-Index Quasi-Monte Carlo (A-MIQMC)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>NEW!</strong> Unbiased Multilevel Monte Carlo (U-MLMC) and Unbiased Multilevel Quasi-Monte Carlo (U-MLQMC)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><strong>NEW!</strong> Unbiased Multi-Index Monte Carlo (U-MIMC) and Unbiased Multi-Index Quasi-Monte Carlo (U-MIQMC)</p>
</li>
</ul>
