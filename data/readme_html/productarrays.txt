<p dir="auto">The ProductArrays is a lazy form of</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="collect(Iterators.product(vectors...))"><pre><span class="pl-c1">collect</span>(Iterators<span class="pl-k">.</span><span class="pl-c1">product</span>(vectors<span class="pl-k">...</span>))</pre></div>
<p dir="auto">i.e. it is an <code>AbstractArray</code> in contrast to <code>Iterators.product(vectors...)</code>. So
is accessible with <code>getindex</code> and gets default Array implementations for free.
In particular it can be passed to <code>Base.PermutedDimsArray</code> for lazy permutation
and <code>vec()</code> to obtain a lazy <code>Base.ReshapedArray</code>.</p>
