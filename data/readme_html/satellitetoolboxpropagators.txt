<p dir="auto">This packages contains orbit propagators for the <strong>SatelliteToolbox.jl</strong> ecosystem.</p>
<p dir="auto">The current supported propagators are:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>J2 analytical orbit propagator;</li>
<li>J2 osculating analytical orbit propagator;</li>
<li>J4 analytical orbit propagator;</li>
<li>J4 osculating analytical orbit propagator;</li>
<li>SGP4/SDP4 orbit propagator; and</li>
<li>Two body analytical orbit propagator.</li>
</ol>
