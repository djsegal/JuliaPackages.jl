<p dir="auto">An important step before developing several gene finding algorithms consist of having a Markov chain representation of the DNA. To do so, we implemented the <code>BioMarkovChain</code> method that will capture the initials and transition probabilities of a DNA sequence (<code>LongSequence</code>) and will create a dedicated object storing relevant information of a DNA Markov chain. Here an example:</p>
