<p dir="auto">To compute the magnetic field components at a given point, use the <code>field_at_point</code> function. The loop normal vector is assumed to be parallel to the <code>z</code> axis.</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="# Define the point at which the magnetic field is computed in Cartesian space

<span class="pl-c"><span class="pl-c">#</span> Compute the field strength, stream function, and scalar potential</span>
