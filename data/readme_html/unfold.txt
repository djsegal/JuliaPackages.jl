<p dir="auto">Toolbox to perform linear / GAM / hierarchical / deconvolution regression on biological signals.</p>
<p dir="auto">This kind of modelling is also known as encoding modeling, linear deconvolution, Temporal Response Functions (TRFs), linear system identification, and probably under other names. fMRI models with HRF-basis functions and pupil-dilation bases are also supported.</p>

<ol dir="auto">
<li>We recommend to write a Literate.jl document and place it in <code>docs/literate/FOLDER/FILENAME.jl</code> with <code>FOLDER</code> being <code>HowTo</code>, <code>Explanation</code>, <code>Tutorial</code> or <code>Reference</code> (<a href="https://documentation.divio.com/" rel="nofollow">recommended reading on the 4 categories</a>).</li>
<li>Literate.jl converts the <code>.jl</code> file to a <code>.md</code> automatically and places it in <code>docs/src/generated/FOLDER/FILENAME.md</code>.</li>
<li>Edit <a href="https://github.com/unfoldtoolbox/Unfold.jl/blob/main/docs/make.jl">make.jl</a> with a reference to <code>docs/src/generated/FOLDER/FILENAME.md</code>.</li>
</ol>
