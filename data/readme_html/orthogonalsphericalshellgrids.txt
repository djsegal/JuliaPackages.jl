<p dir="auto">OrthogonalSphericalShellGrids includes tools for generating spherical shell grids for ocean simulations with Oceananigans.</p>
<p dir="auto">OrthogonalSphericalShellGrids is developed by the <a href="https://clima.caltech.edu" rel="nofollow">Climate Modeling Alliance</a>.</p>
