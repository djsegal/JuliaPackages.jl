<p dir="auto">This package is used to benchmark and interactively explore the results of metagenomic binning given a dataset.
This is the Julia backend of the command-line tool <a href="https://github.com/jakobnissen/BinBencher.jl">BinBencher [work in progress]</a>.</p>
