<p dir="auto">UnitfulData.jl is an extension for <a href="https://github.com/PainterQubits/Unitful.jl">Unitful.jl</a> that includes units of Information commonly encountered in Information Theory and Data Storage.</p>
<p dir="auto">The extension also includes the exported macro <code>@unit_custom_prefix</code> that allows the generation of prefixed units for a general unit using a custom base and exponents and the constant <code>prefixed_data</code> containing the most common units in metric (base 10) and IEC (binary) format.</p>

<p dir="auto">A dimension representing Information either as Storage, Memory, or Entropy.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>bit</code> : the most basic unit of information in computing repesented an a binary digit</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>trit</code>: a ternary unit that encode the information with three non-negative numbers</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>Byte</code>: number of bits used to encode a character of text in the computer.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>sh</code>: the shannon is the unit of information content associated with and event occuring with probability 1/2.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>Hart</code>: the hartley is a unit of information and entropy on base 10</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>nat</code>: the natural unit of information is a unit of information and entropy based on the natural logarithms</p>
</li>
</ul>
