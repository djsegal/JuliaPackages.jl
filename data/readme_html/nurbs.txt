<p dir="auto">This package provides functionality to define and evaluate B-spline, Curry-Schoenberg, and NURBS (non-uniform rational B-spline) basis functions, their derivatives, as well as curves and surfaces based on B-spline and NURBS basis functions.</p>
<p dir="auto">The following aspects are implemented (✓) and planned (⌛):</p>
