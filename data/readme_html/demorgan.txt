<p dir="auto"><em>Classical logic truth table magma algebra</em></p>
<p dir="auto">Truth table expressed as an algebraic magma with operations <code>¬</code>, <code>∧</code>, <code>∨</code>, <code>→</code>, <code>←</code>, <code>↔</code> or alternatively <code>!</code>, <code>&amp;</code>, <code>|</code>, <code>--&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;--</code>, <code>&lt;--&gt;</code>.
Based on classical logic with <code>TruthValues{N}</code> and <code>TruthTable{N,M}</code>, the <code>@truthtable</code> macro assigns named variable propositional projections as generators of the truth.
When <code>using DeMorgan</code> together with <code>PrettyTables</code>, the output is automatically formatted.</p>
