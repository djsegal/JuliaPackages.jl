<ul dir="auto">
<li>count number of words in string</li>
<li>count number of syllables in string</li>
<li>count number of sentences in string</li>
<li>count number of characters in string</li>
<li>count number of complex words in string</li>
<li>count number of polysyllabic words in string</li>
<li>determine average reading time</li>
<li>determine average speaking time</li>
<li>determine difficulty and reading grade level of text</li>
<li>web-based interface that requires zero knowledge of Julia or programming.</li>
</ul>
