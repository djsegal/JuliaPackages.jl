<p dir="auto">Analytic one- and two-particle vertices for the Hubbard atom, taken from Phys. Rev. B 98, 235107 (2018) by Thunström et al.:
<a href="https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.98.235107" rel="nofollow">https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.98.235107</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Available are the functions <code>bare_vertex</code>, <code>gf</code>, <code>chi</code>, <code>chi0</code>, <code>full_vertex</code>, <code>gamma</code>, <code>irreducible_vertex</code>, <code>channel_reducible_vertex</code>, <code>hedin</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">To use these, you need to import <code>SparseIR.jl</code> to be able to create <code>MatsubaraFreq</code> objects.</p>
