<p dir="auto">This package is based on
<a href="https://github.com/gridap/Gridap.jl/tree/master">Gridap.jl</a> to provide
post-processing tools to calculate reconstructed fluxes associated to the given
approximate solution of a PDE.</p>
<p dir="auto">For simplicity, we consider here the Poisson equation</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$$\begin{align}
- \Delta u &amp;= f &amp;&amp;\text{in }\Omega\\\
u &amp;= g &amp;&amp;\text{on }\partial\Omega.
\end{align}$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">We suppose we have already computed a conforming approximation <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$u_h \in
V_h\subset H^1_0(\Omega)$</math-renderer> to the solution <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$u$</math-renderer> in Gridap.jl by solving</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$$(\nabla u_h, \nabla v_h) = (f, v_h)\quad\forall v_h\in V_h,$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">The <code>EquilibratedFlux.jl</code> library then provides the tools to compute a reconstructed flux
associated to <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$u_h$</math-renderer>. This flux, obtained by postprocessing, is an approximation to the numerical flux, i.e.</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$$\sigma_h \approx -\nabla u_h.$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">This flux has the important property of being "conservative over faces" in the
sense that</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$$\sigma_h \in \mathbf{H}(\mathrm{div},\Omega).$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">We provide two functions to obtain such an object:
<a href="https://aerappa.github.io/EquilibratedFlux.jl/dev/#EquilibratedFlux.build_equilibrated_flux-NTuple%7B4,%20Any%7D" rel="nofollow"><code>build_equilibrated_flux</code></a>
and
<a href="https://aerappa.github.io/EquilibratedFlux.jl/dev/#EquilibratedFlux.build_averaged_flux-Tuple%7BAny,%20Any%7D" rel="nofollow"><code>build_averaged_flux</code></a>
both provide reconstructed fluxes, which we denote by <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$\sigma_{\mathrm{eq},h}$</math-renderer>
and <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$\sigma_{\mathrm{ave},h}$</math-renderer> respectively.</p>
<p dir="auto">In addition to the properties listed above, the equilibrated flux
<math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$\sigma_{\mathrm{eq},h}$</math-renderer> satisfies the so-called equilibrium condition, i.e.,
for piecewise polynomial <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$f$</math-renderer>, we have</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="99795d0b87e0e92b5778dd6de93c263a">$$\nabla\cdot\sigma_{\mathrm{eq},h} = f.$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">More details can be found in the <a href="https://aerappa.github.io/EquilibratedFlux.jl/dev/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a>.</p>
