<p dir="auto">Dictionary-based arrays, useful to represent wide heterogeneous tables while enjoying the familiar Julia collection and <code>Tables</code> interfaces.</p>
<p dir="auto">Use <code>DictArrays</code> when you need a lean table type, but the compilation overhead of type-stable solutions (<code>Vector{NamedTuple}</code>, <code>NamedTuple{Vector}</code>, <code>StructArray</code>) is too much.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>DictArray</code>s are similar to <code>StructArrays</code> and have the same interface where possible, with the defining difference that <code>DictArray</code>s do not encode columns in the table type. This get rids of the prohibitive compilation overhead for wide tables with 100s of columns or more.
Despite the inherent type instability, regular Julia data manipulation functions such as <code>map</code> and <code>filter</code> are fast for <code>DictArray</code>s: almost no overhead compared to <code>StructArray</code>s, orders of magnitude faster than plain <code>Vector</code>s of <code>Dict</code>s.</p>

<p dir="auto"><code>DictArrays</code> follow array-like collection interfaces. They are not <code>AbstractArrays</code> though: this is a deliberate decision so that not to trigger generic <code>AbstractArray</code> fallbacks anywhere. Type instability is fundamental to the design, and requires explicit function barriers for performance.</p>
<p dir="auto">Still, lots of common functionality works as you would expect for an array of <code>NamedTuple</code>s: <code>length(da)</code>, <code>da[5]</code>, <code>da[5].colname</code>, <code>keys(da)</code>, <code>map</code>, <code>filter</code>, and others. <code>StructArray</code>-like behavior is also available with the same semantics: most notably, <code>da.colname</code> to retrieve the whole column.</p>

<p dir="auto">Conversion:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>Dictionary(da)</code> retrieves the underlying dictionary of columns</li>
<li><code>Dict(da)</code>, <code>NamedTuple(da)</code> convert to the corresponding type</li>
<li><code>StructArray(da)</code> converts to a <code>StructArray</code> of <code>NamedTuples</code> without copying columns</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Modification: uses <code>Accessors</code>, same interface as <code>StructArray</code>.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>@set da.colname = 1:length(da)</code> replace a column</li>
<li><code>@insert da.colname = ...</code> insert a new column</li>
<li><code>@delete da.colname</code> delete a column</li>
<li><code>Properties()</code> are supported. Eg, normalize all numeric columns:</li>
</ul>
