<p dir="auto">UtilityModels.jl is a collection of utility based decision models. Currently, expected utlity theory, transfer of attention exchange, and prospect theory are implemented. More models soon to follow. See the <a href="https://itsdfish.github.io/UtilityModels.jl/dev/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> for more details.</p>
