<p dir="auto">This package provides the functions for extracting object wavefront from interferograms based on the phase-shifting method.
Two [1], three [2,3], four [2,3], parallel [4,5], and generalized [2,3] phase-shifting methods are implemented.
For detailed principles, please refer to the following references.</p>
<blockquote>
<ol dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.31.001414" rel="nofollow">X. F. Meng, L. Z. Cai, X. F. Xu, X. L. Yang, X. X. Shen, G. Y. Dong, and Y. R. Wang, "Two-step phase-shifting interferometry and its application in image encryption," Opt. Lett. <strong>31</strong>, 1414-1416 (2006)</a></li>
<li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S0079-6638(08)70178-1" rel="nofollow">Katherine Creath, "V Phase-Measurement Interferometry Techniques," Progress in Optics <strong>26</strong>, 349-393 (1988)</a></li>
<li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470135976.ch14" rel="nofollow">Horst Schreiber, and John H. Bruning, "Phase Shifting Interferometry," in Daniel Malacara (ed.), <em>Optical Shop Testing</em>, John Wiley &amp; Sons, Ltd, pp. 547-666 (2006)</a></li>
<li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1777796" rel="nofollow">Y. Awatsuji, M. Sasada, and T. Kubota, "Parallel quasi-phase-shifting digital holography," Appl. Phys. Lett., <strong>85</strong>, 1069-1071 (2004).</a></li>
<li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/9781118705766.ch1" rel="nofollow">Yasuhiro Awatsuji, "Parallel Phase-Shifting Digital Holography," in Bahram Javidi, Enrique Tajahuerce, Pedro Andrés (eds.), <em>Multi-Dimensional Imaging</em>, John Wiley &amp; Sons, Ltd, pp. 1-23</a></li>
</ol>
</blockquote>
