<p dir="auto">
This package is used for generating structured points for Material Point Method (MPM) simulation. Currently, there are some limitations in this package, please follow the <a href="https://LandslideSIM.github.io/MaterialPointGenerator.jl/stable" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> step-by-step to reproduce the results.
</p>
