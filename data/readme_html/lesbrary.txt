<p dir="auto"><a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7997001" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://camo.githubusercontent.com/52d7bafcfc66bacb33a5460c94bf0fd25594f5b1b1822fc3fa7f2de46ca64b5b/68747470733a2f2f7a656e6f646f2e6f72672f62616467652f444f492f31302e353238312f7a656e6f646f2e373939373030312e737667" alt="DOI" data-canonical-src="https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.7997001.svg" style="max-width: 100%;"></a></p>
<p dir="auto">This package is a framework for building a library of large eddy simulations (LES) of ocean surface boundary layer turbulence — the <em>LESbrary</em> — with <a href="https://github.com/climate-machine/Oceananigans.jl">Oceananigans.jl</a>. The LESbrary will archive turbulence data for both idealized and realistic oceanic scenarios.</p>
<p dir="auto">LESbrary.jl is developed by the <a href="https://clima.caltech.edu" rel="nofollow">Climate Modeling Alliance</a> and collaborators.</p>

<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="julia&gt; using Pkg
