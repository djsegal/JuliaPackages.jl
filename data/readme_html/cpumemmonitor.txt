<p dir="auto">Using the following function, two files, named <code>$fn.cpu</code> and <code>$fn.mem</code> will be used to save cpu and mem info, respectively.</p>
<p dir="auto">The info will be recorded every <code>interval</code> seconds for <code>iterations</code> times, and wait for another <code>outer_interval</code> seconds to repeat.</p>
<p dir="auto">This process will end if the corresponding <code>pid</code> does not exist.</p>
