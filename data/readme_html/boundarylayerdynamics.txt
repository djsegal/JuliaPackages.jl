<p dir="auto"><em>Turbulence-resolving simulations of boundary-layer flows.</em></p>
<p dir="auto">BoundaryLayerDynamics.jl can simulate a range of physical problems, but its primary purpose is to simulate the evolution of turbulent flows in simplified representations of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_boundary_layer" rel="nofollow">atmospheric boundary layer</a>.
It can be used to perform <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_numerical_simulation" rel="nofollow">direct numerical simulation (DNS)</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_eddy_simulation" rel="nofollow">large-eddy simulation (LES)</a> of flows in channel-like geometries.
A detailed description of the physical and mathematical models is provided in <a href="https://docs.mfsch.dev/BoundaryLayerDynamics.jl/" rel="nofollow">the documentation</a> and in <a href="https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2023-1071" rel="nofollow">a preprint</a> describing the code.</p>
