<p dir="auto"><code>InverseLangevinApproximations.jl</code> is a library for utilizing different approximation of the inverse of the Langevin function, ℒ. The approximations are primarily used in polymer mechanics; however, due to the potential use of the function in other areas of physics, the library is not limited to polymer mechanics.</p>

<p dir="auto">All approximations are subtypes of <code>AbstractInverseLangevinApproximation</code>. Accessing the inverse of the Langevin function is done by calling <code>inverse_langevin_approximation</code> with the approximation type as the first argument and the value to approximate as the second argument.</p>
