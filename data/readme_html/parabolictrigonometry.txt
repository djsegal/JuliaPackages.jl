<p dir="auto">The parabolic trigonometric functions <code>sinp</code> and <code>cosp</code>, the neglected
cousins of <code>sin</code>, <code>cos</code>, <code>sinh</code>, and <code>cosh</code>. Modelled after
G. Dattoli, M. Migliorati, M. Quattromini, and P. E. Ricci,
<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1563" rel="nofollow">arXiv:1102.1563 [math-ph]</a>, and
beautifully explained by Michael Penn <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uIgt-H7l5As" rel="nofollow">in a
video</a>.</p>
