<p dir="auto">A quick realization of qubit-efficient quantum circuit architecture of Matrix Product States (QMPS). This package is an extension package for a quantum simulation framework called <a href="https://github.com/QuantumBFS/Yao.jl"><strong>Yao</strong></a>.</p>

<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>DCbuilder</code>: Generate the structure of elements may needed for a Quantum differentiable circuit.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>MPSDCpar</code>: Get the circuit parameters of a differentiable QMPS circuit (QMPS-DC) or of a QMPS-DC extended circuit.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>markDiff</code>: Return the differentiable gate(s) <code>QMPS.QDiff{GT, N}</code> from a block or a block tree such as <code>ChainBlock</code>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>getQdiff</code>: <a href="#jump">Quantum differentiation.</a></p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>getNdiff</code>: Numerical differentiation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
