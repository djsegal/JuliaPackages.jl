<p dir="auto">Both the <code>DP5Solver</code> and <code>DP8Solver</code>'s are stateful allowing for memory-efficient integration to future end times from the last integrated end point (e.g. if you chose t = 1.0 as your endpoint you can call <code>integrate!</code> again with t=2.0 and it will "carry forward" the work starting from t = 1.0 instead of requiring you to set things up all over again).</p>

        end
