<p dir="auto">SpinWeightedSpheroidalHarmonics.jl computes spin-weighted spheroidal harmonics and eigenvalues using a spectral decomposition method.</p>
<p dir="auto">Note that v0.5.0 is a <em>breaking</em> release.</p>
