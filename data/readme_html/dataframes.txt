<p dir="auto"><strong>Maintenance</strong>: DataFrames is maintained collectively by the <a href="https://github.com/orgs/JuliaData/people">JuliaData collaborators</a>.
Responsiveness to pull requests and issues can vary, depending on the availability of key collaborators.</p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Learning</strong>: New to DataFrames.jl? Check out our
<a href="https://juliaacademy.com/p/introduction-to-dataframes-jl" rel="nofollow">free Julia Academy course</a>
which will walk you through how to use DataFrames.jl.
You can also check out
<a href="https://github.com/bkamins/Julia-DataFrames-Tutorial">Bogumił Kamiński's DataFrames.jl tutorial</a>
that is available on GitHub.</p>
