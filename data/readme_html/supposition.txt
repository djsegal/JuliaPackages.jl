<p dir="auto">This is a Julia implementation of property based testing. It's been heavily inspired by the testing framework <a href="https://hypothesis.readthedocs.io/en/latest/" rel="nofollow">Hypothesis</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">If you haven't heard of property based testing before or you're not (yet) convinced that it can be a boon, check out <a href="https://arialdomartini.github.io/property-testing" rel="nofollow">Property-based Testing For The Rest Of Us (or: The Natural Next Step After TDD)</a>
by Arialdo Martini. The series gives a very gentle introduction as to WHY property based testing is a good thing, and to acknowledge that it's
usually pretty hard to do. The examples used are for C#, but the concepts explained there have their equivalents in Supposition.jl.</p>

<p dir="auto">Supposition.jl features the following capabilities:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Shrinking of generated examples
<ul dir="auto">
<li>targeted shrinking, counterexample shrinking and error-based shrinking are all supported</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Combination of generators into new ones</li>
<li>Basic stateful testing</li>
<li>Deterministic replaying of previously recorded counterexamples</li>
<li>Integration into existing frameworks through <code>Test.AbstractTestset</code></li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Please check out the <a href="https://seelengrab.github.io/Supposition.jl/stable" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> for more information on how to use Supposition.jl!</p>
<p dir="auto">If you have specific usage questions, ideas for new features or want to show off
your fuzzing skills, please share it on the <a href="https://github.com/Seelengrab/Supposition.jl/discussions">Discussions Tab</a>!</p>
