<p dir="auto"><strong>PDEngine.jl</strong> is a Julia library designed for solving partial differential equations (PDEs) using a variety of numerical methods. This library aims to provide efficient, flexible, and easy-to-use solvers for some of the most common PDEs encountered in scientific and engineering problems.
<a href="https://jakubschwenkbeck.github.io/PDEngine.jl/" rel="nofollow">Documentation</a></p>

<ul dir="auto">
<li><strong>Heat Equation</strong>: Models heat distribution over time.</li>
<li><strong>Wave Equation</strong>: Simulates the propagation of waves through a medium.</li>
<li><strong>Navier-Stokes Equations</strong>: Governs the motion of viscous fluid substances.</li>
<li><strong>Poisson's Equation</strong>: Used in electrostatics, mechanical engineering, and theoretical physics.</li>
</ul>

<ul dir="auto">
<li><strong>Finite Difference Method (FDM)</strong>: A straightforward approach to discretize PDEs on regular grids.</li>
<li><strong>Finite Element Method (FEM)</strong>: Ideal for complex geometries and adaptive mesh refinement.</li>
<li><strong>Spectral Methods</strong>: Provides high accuracy for smooth solutions and periodic boundary conditions.</li>
</ul>
