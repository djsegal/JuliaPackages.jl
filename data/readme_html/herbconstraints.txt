<p dir="auto">This package contains the functionality to formulate, represent and use constraints within <code>Herb</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Constraints are formulated as context-sensitive grammars. Further, they are divided into global and local constraints, that may be partially mapped to each other.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>HerbConstraints.jl</code> provides functionality to propagate constraints and match constraint patterns efficiently. Further, provides error handling through <code>matchfail</code> and <code>matchnode</code>.</p>
