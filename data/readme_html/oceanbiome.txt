<p dir="auto">OceanBioME is a flexible biogeochemical modelling environment written in Julia for modelling the coupled interactions between ocean biology, carbonate chemistry, and physics. OceanBioME can be run as a stand-alone box model, or coupled with <a href="https://github.com/cliMA/oceananigans.jl/">Oceananigans.jl</a> to run as a 1D column model or with 2 and 3D physics.</p>
<p dir="auto">OceanBioME was developed with generous support from the Centre for Climate Repair <a href="https://www.climaterepair.cam.ac.uk" rel="nofollow">CCR</a> and the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation as a tool to study the effectiveness and impacts of ocean carbon dioxide removal (CDR) strategies.</p>
