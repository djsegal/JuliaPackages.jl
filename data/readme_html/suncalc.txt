<p dir="auto">Julia implementation of the <a href="https://github.com/mourner/suncalc/">SunCalc</a> package for
calculating sun position, sunlight phases (times for sunrise, sunset, dusk, etc.),
moon position, and lunar phase for the given location and time.</p>
<p dir="auto">Most calculations are based on the formulas given in the Astronomy Answers articles
about <a href="https://www.aa.quae.nl/en/reken/zonpositie.html" rel="nofollow">position of the sun</a>
and <a href="https://www.aa.quae.nl/en/reken/hemelpositie.html" rel="nofollow">the planets</a>.
You can find more information on twilight phases calculated by SunCalc.jl
in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight" rel="nofollow">Twilight article on Wikipedia</a>.</p>
