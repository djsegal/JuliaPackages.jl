<p dir="auto"><code>Thyrosim.jl</code> is an updated version of <a href="http://biocyb1.cs.ucla.edu/thyrosim/cgi-bin/Thyrosim.cgi" rel="nofollow">THYROSIM</a>. It heavily relies on the amazing <a href="https://github.com/SciML/DifferentialEquations.jl">DifferentialEquations.jl</a> package to solve systems of ODEs.</p>
<p dir="auto">This tool is developed to</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>Better optimize replacement LT4 and LT4+LT3 dosing for treating hypothyroid patients, based on their individual hormone levels, heights, weights and gender</li>
<li>Aid in understanding more precisely how gender and BMI impact thyroid hormone transient and steady state dynamical regulation over time in these patients.</li>
</ol>
