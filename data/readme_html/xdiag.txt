<ul dir="auto">
<li>Basic algebra of operators in quantum many-body systems</li>
<li>Iterative linear algebra for computing eigendecompositions and time-evolutions (e.g. Lanczos algorithm)</li>
<li>Local spin, t-J, or fermionic models</li>
<li>Full support of generic space group symmetries</li>
<li>parallelization using OpenMP</li>
<li>based on C++ library <a href="https://github.com/awietek/xdiag">xdiag</a></li>
</ul>
