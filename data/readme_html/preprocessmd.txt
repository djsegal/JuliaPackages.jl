<p dir="auto">The purpose of <code>PreprocessMD.jl</code> is to provide a suite of functions for preprocesing biomedical data.
The scope of this package is <em><strong>medical data preprocessing</strong></em>, so
we develop functions that are specific to biomedical research but general enough for widespread use.
These tools are developed for the OMOP Common Data Model<sup><a href="#user-content-fn-OMOP-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" id="user-content-fnref-omop-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" data-footnote-ref="" aria-describedby="footnote-label">1</a></sup>,
especially the MIMIC-IV demo set<sup><a href="#user-content-fn-MIMIC-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" id="user-content-fnref-mimic-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" data-footnote-ref="" aria-describedby="footnote-label">2</a></sup>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Following the definitions of Hu et al.<sup><a href="#user-content-fn-Wu-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" id="user-content-fnref-wu-8182f55ec4c5ee6a096455e164589193" data-footnote-ref="" aria-describedby="footnote-label">3</a></sup>, we consider <em><strong>data preprocessing</strong></em> to include project-level data manipulations,
as opposed to the upstream <em><strong>data cleaning</strong></em> (e.g., error-corrections and standardizations) that is typically performed over an entire database,
and the downstream <em><strong>data preparing</strong></em> (e.g., labelling and classification), which might vary across any number of analyses within a project.</p>
