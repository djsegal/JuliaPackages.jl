<p dir="auto"><strong>StatusSwitchingQP.jl</strong> solves the following convex quadratic programming problems (called <code>SSQP</code>):</p>
<p dir="auto"><math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="f3143b70645bdcf705ea516e21537a31">$$
\begin{array}
[c]{cl}
\min &amp; \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{x}^{\prime}\mathbf{Vx}+\mathbf{x}^{\prime}
\mathbf{q}\\
s.t. &amp; \mathbf{Ax}=\mathbf{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{M}\\
&amp; \mathbf{Gx}\leq\mathbf{g}\in\mathbb{R}^{J}\\
&amp; \boldsymbol{d}\leq\mathbf{x}\leq\boldsymbol{u}\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
\end{array}
$$</math-renderer></p>
<p dir="auto">with positive semi-definite symmetric matrix <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="f3143b70645bdcf705ea516e21537a31">$\mathbf{V}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times N}$</math-renderer>.</p>
