<p dir="auto">This project aims to provide the functionalities of the
<a href="https://de.maplesoft.com/products/Maple/index.aspx" rel="nofollow">Maple</a> part of
<a href="http://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/CHEVIE/index.html" rel="nofollow">CHEVIE</a> in
<a href="https://julialang.org/" rel="nofollow">Julia</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Currently the following functionalities are missing:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>PrintValPhi</li>
<li>GreenFunctionsA</li>
<li>GreenFunctionsA2</li>
<li>PrintToTex (partially)</li>
</ul>
