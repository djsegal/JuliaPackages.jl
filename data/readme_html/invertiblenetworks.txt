<p dir="auto">Building blocks for invertible neural networks in the <a href="https://julialang.org" rel="nofollow">Julia</a> programming language.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Memory efficient building blocks for invertible neural networks</li>
<li>Hand-derived gradients, Jacobians <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="13b1161b75f791b32de01a35aff7c261">$J$</math-renderer> , and <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="13b1161b75f791b32de01a35aff7c261">$\log |J|$</math-renderer>
</li>
<li>
<a href="https://fluxml.ai" rel="nofollow">Flux</a> integration</li>
<li>Support for <a href="https://github.com/FluxML/Zygote.jl">Zygote</a> and <a href="https://github.com/JuliaDiff/ChainRules.jl">ChainRules</a>
</li>
<li>GPU support</li>
<li>Includes various examples of invertible neural networks, normalizing flows, variational inference, and uncertainty quantification</li>
</ul>

<p dir="auto">Due to its memory scaling InvertibleNetworks.jl, has been particularily successful at Bayesian posterior sampling with simulation-based inference. To get started with this application refer to a simple example (<a href="https://github.com/slimgroup/InvertibleNetworks.jl/tree/master/examples/applications/conditional_sampling/amortized_glow_mnist_inpainting.jl">Conditional sampling for MNSIT inpainting</a>) but feel free to modify this script for your application and please reach out to us for help.</p>
