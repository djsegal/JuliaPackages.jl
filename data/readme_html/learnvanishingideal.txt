<p dir="auto">This <code>Julia</code> package aims at learning an algebraic variety from a list of potentially noisy data points. In doing so, it uses statistical techniques inspired by the <em>Generalized Principal Component Analysis</em> by Ma et al.</p>
<p dir="auto">It runs under the assumption that the underlying variety is a complete intersection. At the moment, either a maximum occuring degree among the generators of the vanishing ideal or a list of degrees is required as input. In addition, we assume that the codimension of the variety is known.</p>
