<p dir="auto">WTP.jl provides a <strong>thin layer of abstractions</strong> for processing condense-phase
orbitals. The library aims primarily to save ourselves from the insufferable
agony of indices gymnastics without immolating all the performance.  Our goal
is to make condense-phase codes  more concise and comprehensible with a
tolerable performance sacrifice.</p>
<p dir="auto">The software design of <code>WTP.jl</code> follows the way of a computer scientist (as
opposed to that of a computational scientist). I'm approaching numerical
software through thin layers of composible abstractions instead of a large
<code>code</code> with an extensive features and optimization.</p>
<p dir="auto">Currently, we provide a few functionalities.</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>Nonorthogonal and periodic grids: Support intuitive indexing, iteration, and Fourier transforms. This encapsulates the crystal lattice, the reciprocal lattice, the Brillouin zone, and the homecell.</li>
<li>Orbitals: We provide an intuitive interface for efficient inner products, fft, various forms of indexing, folding, and translation.</li>
<li>Some IO functionalities with Quantum Espresso's <code>wfc?.dat</code> files and
Wannier90's <code>.amn</code>/<code>.mmn</code> files. WTP reads these files and associates them with a mesh for easy indexing.</li>
</ol>
