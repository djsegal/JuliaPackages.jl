<p dir="auto">Toolbox for quantum information, nonlocality, and entanglement.</p>
<p dir="auto">Highlights are the functions <code>mub</code> and <code>sic_povm</code>, that produce respectively MUBs and SIC-POVMs with arbitrary precision, <code>local_bound</code> that uses a parallelized algorithm to compute the local bound of a Bell inequality, and <code>partial_trace</code> and <code>partial_transpose</code>, that compute the partial trace and partial transpose in a way that can be used for optimization with <a href="https://jump.dev/JuMP.jl/stable/" rel="nofollow">JuMP</a>. Also worth mentioning are the functions to produce uniformly-distributed random states, unitaries, and POVMs: <code>random_state</code>, <code>random_unitary</code>, <code>random_povm</code>. And the eponymous <code>ket</code>, of course.</p>
<p dir="auto">For the full list of functions see the <a href="https://araujoms.github.io/Ket.jl/dev/api/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a>.</p>
