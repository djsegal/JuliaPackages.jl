<p dir="auto">The current version is highly stable and you can confidently use it for your projects.
In the immediate future, we will <strong>not</strong> be introducing new features to the framework. Our focus is on maintaining its stability and robustness, ensuring that it stands resilient in various use-cases and scenarios.</p>
