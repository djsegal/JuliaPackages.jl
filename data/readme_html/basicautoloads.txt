<p dir="auto">BasicAutoloads lets you say "whenever I type this in the REPL, run that for me". It's great
for automatically loading interactive tools.</p>
<p dir="auto">For example, put this in your startup.jl</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="if isinteractive()

        [&quot;@b&quot;, &quot;@be&quot;]            =&gt; :(using Chairmarks),
        [&quot;@benchmark&quot;]           =&gt; :(using BenchmarkTools),
        [&quot;@test&quot;, &quot;@testset&quot;, &quot;@test_broken&quot;, &quot;@test_deprecated&quot;, &quot;@test_logs&quot;,
        &quot;@test_nowarn&quot;, &quot;@test_skip&quot;, &quot;@test_throws&quot;, &quot;@test_warn&quot;, &quot;@inferred&quot;] =&gt;
                                    :(using Test),
        [&quot;@about&quot;]               =&gt; :(using About; macro about(x) Expr(:call, About.about, x) end),
    ])

<p dir="auto">Add <code>BasicAutoloads</code> and any packages you want to automatically load to your default
environment, and then enjoy the benefits at the REPL:</p>
