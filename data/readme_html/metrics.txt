<p dir="auto">A collection of diverse metrics to analyse performance of Machine Learning and Deep Learning Models. This includes a variety of functions for <code>Classification</code>, <code>Regression</code>, <code>Natural Language Processing</code>, <code>Computer Vision</code> and <code>Ranking</code> Models and also utilities for better user support.</p>
