<p dir="auto"><strong>GeoStats.jl</strong> is an extensible framework for <strong>geospatial data science</strong> and
<strong>geostatistical modeling</strong> fully written in Julia. It is comprised of several
modules for advanced geometric processing, state-of-the-art geostatistical
algorithms and sophisticated visualization of geospatial data.</p>
<p dir="auto">All further information is provided in the
<a href="https://JuliaEarth.github.io/GeoStatsDocs/stable" rel="nofollow">online documentation</a>.</p>
