<p dir="auto"><strong>IDFDataCanada.jl</strong> offers methods to get Intensity-Duration-Frequency (IDF) data from Engineering Climate Datasets of Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) in CSV (<code>.csv</code>) or NetCDF (<code>.nc</code>) from the <code>.txt</code> files via automatic download and extraction from <code>.zip</code> archives on <a href="https://collaboration.cmc.ec.gc.ca/cmc/climate/Engineer_Climate/IDF/" rel="nofollow">ECCC's server</a>.</p>
