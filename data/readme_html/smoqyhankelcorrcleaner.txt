<p dir="auto"><a href="https://github.com/SmoQySuite/SmoQyHankelCorrCleaner.jl">SmoQyHankelCorrCleaner.jl</a> implements the algorithm introduced
in the paper <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12349" rel="nofollow">"Denoising of Imaginary Time Response Functions with Hankel Projections"</a>
for denoising imaginary time correlation data, the citation for which is given below:</p>
