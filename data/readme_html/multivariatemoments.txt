<p dir="auto">Extension of <a href="https://github.com/JuliaAlgebra/MultivariatePolynomials.jl">MultivariatePolynomials</a> to moments of multivariate measures and their scalar product with polynomials.
It also includes the extraction of atomic measures from moment matrices which is related to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_problem" rel="nofollow">moment problem</a>.</p>
