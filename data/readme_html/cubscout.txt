<p dir="auto"><strong>C</strong>odon <strong>U</strong>sage <strong>B</strong>ias (CUB) in Julia</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>CUBScout</code> helps you work with codons! Beyond counting codons and finding codon frequency, <code>CUBScout</code> calculates <strong>C</strong>odon <strong>U</strong>sage <strong>B</strong>ias (CUB) and related expressivity predictions. Currently, <code>CUBScout</code> calculates:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Six measures of codon usage bias:
<ul dir="auto">
<li>B, from Karlin and Mrazek, 1996</li>
<li>ENC, from Wright 1990</li>
<li>ENC', from Novembre, 2002</li>
<li>MCB, from Urrutia and Hurst, 2001</li>
<li>MILC, from Supek and Vlahovicek, 2005</li>
<li>SCUO, from Wan et al., 2004</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Five expressivity measures based on codon usage bias:
<ul dir="auto">
<li>CAI, from Sharp and Li, 1987</li>
<li>E, from Karlin and Mrazek, 1996</li>
<li>FOP, from Ikemura, 1981</li>
<li>GCB, from Merkl, 2003</li>
<li>MELP, from Supek and Vlahovicek, 2005</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto"><code>CUBScout</code> is based off of the fabulous <a href="https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/coRdon.html" rel="nofollow">coRdon</a> package in R by Anamaria Elek, Maja Kuzman, and Kristian Vlahovicek. I am grateful for their clear code and would encourage you to cite coRdon as well when using <code>CUBScout</code>.</p>

<p dir="auto">CUBScout is under active development, and I welcome contributions or suggestions! Additional features I'm working on/would like to incorporate:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Performance improvements</li>
<li>Plotting support (e.g. BPlots)</li>
<li>Additional CUB measures, including S, RCDI, CDC, RCA, RCSU, and RCBS</li>
<li>Growth predictions derived from CUB, such as those in growthpred and gRodon</li>
</ul>
