<p dir="auto">Continuous transformations (or more precisely, homeomorphisms) from ℝ (and two-point compactified version) and ℝⁿ to various open (or closed) sets used in statistics and numerical methods, such as intervals, simplexes, ordered vectors.</p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Work in progress, API may change without notice.</strong></p>

<p dir="auto">This package was born because I was tired of coding the same transformations over and over, with occasional bugs, and wanted something well-tested.</p>
<p dir="auto">Transformations defined by the package can be</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li>called as functions,</li>
<li>provide the <code>logjac(transformation, x)</code> method for the log Jacobian determinant,</li>
<li>prodide the <code>inverse(transformation, x)</code> method for the inverse.</li>
</ol>
<p dir="auto">Log jacobian determinants and their log are useful for domain transformations in MCMC, among other things.</p>
<p dir="auto">In addition, the package includes types to represent intervals, and some basic methods of working with them. The concept of intervals is slightly different from <a href="https://github.com/JuliaMath/IntervalSets.jl">IntervalSet.jl</a> and <a href="https://github.com/dpsanders/ValidatedNumerics.jl">ValidatedNumerics.jl</a>, and as a result not compatible with either.</p>
<p dir="auto">The convenience function <code>bridge(dom, img)</code> figures out the right transformation from <code>dom</code> to <code>img</code>. Currently implemented for intervals.</p>
<p dir="auto">Examples:</p>

<p dir="auto"><code>ArrayTransformation(transformation, dimensions...)</code> transforms a vector of numbers to an array elementwise using <code>transformation</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>TransformationTuple(transformations)</code> can be used for heterogeneous collections of transformations.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>TransformLogLikelihood</code> wraps a log likelihood function, and <code>TransformDistribution</code> transforms a distribution. Both of them take care of the log Jacobian determinant adjustment.</p>
<p dir="auto">Special transformations, useful for Bayesian methods, are also available (WIP). Feature and pull requests are appreciated.</p>
