<p dir="auto">A package for optimising the structural parameters of an atomistic system,
i.e. the step usually referred to as
<strong>geometry optimisation</strong> or <strong>structural relaxation</strong>
in electronic structure theory and atomistic modelling.</p>
<p dir="auto">The package is generic in the datastructures used to represent the geometry,
the calculator used to evaluate energies and forces as well as the solver algorithm.
Generally all
<a href="https://github.com/JuliaMolSim/AtomsBase.jl">AtomsBase structures</a>
and <a href="https://github.com/JuliaMolSim/AtomsCalculators.jl">AtomsCalculator calculators</a>
should work out of the box.</p>
<p dir="auto">See the <a href="https://JuliaMolSim.github.io/GeometryOptimization.jl/stable/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a>
for examples and further details.</p>
