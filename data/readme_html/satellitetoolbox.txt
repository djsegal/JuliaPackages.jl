<p dir="auto">The <strong>SatelliteToolbox.jl</strong> contains a set of packages with functions to perform analysis
and build simulations related to satellites. It is used on a daily basis on projects at the
<a href="http://www.gov.br/inpe" rel="nofollow">Brazilian National Institute for Space Research (INPE)</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">The set of packages are listed bellow. All of them are loaded and reexported in this one.</p>
