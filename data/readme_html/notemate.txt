<p dir="auto">The perfect companion for working with your notes 📚</p>
</blockquote>
<p dir="auto"><code>NoteMate.jl</code> is a programmatic transformation tool used to parse and manipulate notes whose structure follows the <a href="https://jacobzelko.com/04172022033744-open-knowledge-standard/index.html" rel="nofollow">Open Knowledge Model (OKM)</a>.
The goal of <code>NoteMate.jl</code> is to enable one to focus only on writing without worry of needing to add any special markup to your note <em>while</em> having the benefits of quickly sharing thoughts.
In fact, sharing your thoughts, whether locally or on the internet, is never far away as <code>NoteMate.jl</code> provides tools to enable your thoughts to be published on <a href="https://www.lekoarts.de/garden/what-is-a-digital-garden" rel="nofollow">digital gardens</a>.
Finally, due to the way <code>NoteMate.jl</code> parses documents, <code>NoteMate.jl</code> can transform notes following the OKM and express them in different outputs irrespective of the implementation.</p>

<p dir="auto">Currently, these are some of the high level capabilities <code>NoteMate.jl</code> can perform:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto">Ingest supported filetypes following OKM layout:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Markdown</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Parsing of a OKM "Note" object to different support outputs:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://franklinjl.org" rel="nofollow"><code>Franklin.jl</code></a> Markdown</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Static site deployment workflows supported for rapid <a href="https://www.lekoarts.de/garden/what-is-a-digital-garden" rel="nofollow">digital garden</a> creation:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://franklinjl.org" rel="nofollow"><code>Franklin.jl</code></a> Support</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">Custom citation rendering and creation via <code>pandoc</code>, <code>bibtex</code>, and <code>CSL</code></p>
</li>
</ul>
