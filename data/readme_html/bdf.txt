<p dir="auto"><code>BDF.jl</code> is a Julia module to read/write BIOSEMI 24-bit <a href="http://www.biosemi.com/faq/file_format.htm" rel="nofollow">BDF</a> files (used for storing electroencephalographic recordings)</p>
<p dir="auto">Usage:</p>
