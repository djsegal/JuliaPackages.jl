<p dir="auto">Features</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Resolves relative links</li>
<li>Heuristics to determine what is and is not a link</li>
<li>Handles malformed web-pages gracefully</li>
<li>Low compile and precompile times</li>
<li>Option to avoid web query if you provide the body of the page as a keyword argument</li>
<li>Filters out duplicates</li>
</ul>
