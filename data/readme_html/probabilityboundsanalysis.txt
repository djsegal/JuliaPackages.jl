<p dir="auto"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_bounds_analysis" rel="nofollow">Probability bounds analysis</a> in Julia, a package for performing arithmetic between uncertain numbers. <code>ProbabilityBoundsAnalysis.jl</code> computes guaranteed bounds on functions of random variables, given only partial information about their marginals and dependence. Considered to be a form of rigorous computing with random variables.</p>

<p dir="auto">Supported uncertain numbers:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>scalars</li>
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulative_distribution_function" rel="nofollow">probability distributions</a></li>
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interval_arithmetic" rel="nofollow">intervals</a></li>
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_box" rel="nofollow">probability boxes</a> (p-boxes)</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">For more information and use, please see the <a href="https://andergray.github.io/ProbabilityBoundsAnalysis.jl/dev/" rel="nofollow">docs</a></p>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZndC2YXa-t0&amp;t=1s" rel="nofollow">JuliaCon 2021 video</a></p>
