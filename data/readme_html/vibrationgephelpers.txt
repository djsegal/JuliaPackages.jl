<p dir="auto">Vibration Generalized Eigenvalue Problem (GEP) helper functions.</p>
<p dir="auto">Targeted at matrix pencils with real symmetric matrices. The GEP reads</p>
<p dir="auto">(K - omega^2 * M) * v = 0</p>
<p dir="auto">Here K is a symmetric, positive semi-definite, stiffness matrix, M is a
symmetric positive definite mass matrix, omega is the angular velocity.</p>
