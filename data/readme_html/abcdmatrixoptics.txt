<p dir="auto">This package implements the linear tracing of simple optical systems based on the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_transfer_matrix_analysis" rel="nofollow">Ray transfer matrix analysis</a>.
The convention of the optical elements is identical to those on Wikipedia. So far we allow most standard optical elements and geometric and Gaussian beams.
If you need another element, feel free to open an issue!</p>
