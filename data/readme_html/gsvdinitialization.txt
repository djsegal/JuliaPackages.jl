<p dir="auto">This package implements the technique in the paper <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08260" rel="nofollow">GSVD-NMF: Recovering Missing Features in
Non-negative Matrix Factorization</a>.
It is used to recover Non-negative matrix factorization(NMF) components from an initial lower-rank factorization by exploiting the generalized singular value decomposition (GSVD) between existing NMF results and the SVD of X.
This method allows the incremental expansion of the number of components, which can be convenient and effective for interactive analysis of large-scale data.</p>
<p dir="auto">See also <a href="https://github.com/HolyLab/NMFMerge.jl">NMFMerge</a> for the converse operation. Together, the two result in a substantial improvement in the quality and consistency of NMF factorization.</p>

<p dir="auto">W, H = <strong>gsvdnmf</strong>(X::AbstractMatrix, ncomponents::Pair{Int,Int};
tol_final=1e-4,
tol_intermediate=1e-4,
kwargs...)</p>
<p dir="auto">Perform "GSVD-NMF" on the data matrix <code>X</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Arguments:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>X</code>: non-negative data matrix</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>ncomponents</code>: in the form of <code>n1 =&gt; n2</code>, augments from <code>n1</code> components to <code>n2</code>components,
where <code>n1</code> is the number of components for initial NMF (under-complete NMF), and <code>n2</code> is the number of
components for final NMF.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Alternatively, <code>ncomponents</code> can be an integer denoting the number of components for final NMF.
In this case, <code>gsvdnmf</code> defaults to augment components on initial NMF solution by 1.</p>
<p dir="auto">Keyword arguments:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>tol_final</code>: The tolerence of final NMF, default:<code>10^{-4}</code></p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>tol_intermediate</code>: The tolerence of initial NMF (under-complete NMF), default: tol_final</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Other keyword arguments are passed to <code>NMF.nnmf</code>.</p>
<hr>
<p dir="auto">W, H = <strong>gsvdnmf</strong>(X::AbstractMatrix, W::AbstractMatrix, H::AbstractMatrix, f;
n2 = size(first(f), 2),
tol_nmf=1e-4,
kwargs...)</p>
<p dir="auto">Augment <code>W</code> and <code>H</code> to have <code>n2</code> components, subsequently polished by NMF.</p>
<p dir="auto">Arguments:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>X</code>: non-negative data matrix</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>W</code> and <code>H</code>: initial NMF factorization</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>n2</code>: the number of components in augmented factorization</p>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto"><code>f</code>: SVD (or Truncated SVD) of <code>X</code></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Keyword arguments:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>tol_nmf</code>: the tolerance of  NMF polishing step, default: 1e-4</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Other keyword arguments are passed to <code>NMF.nnmf</code>.</p>
<hr>
<p dir="auto">Wadd, Hadd, S = <strong>gsvdrecover</strong>(X, W0, H0, kadd, f)</p>
<p dir="auto">Augment components for <code>W</code> and <code>H</code> without polishing by NMF.</p>
<p dir="auto">Outputs:</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>Wadd</code>: augmented NMF solution</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>Hadd</code>: augmented NMF solution</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>S</code>: generalized singular values for the <code>kadd</code> augmented components</p>
<p dir="auto">Arguments:</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>X</code>: non-nagetive 2D data matrix</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>W0</code>: NMF solution</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>H0</code>: NMF solution</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>kadd</code>: number of new components</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>f</code>: SVD (or Truncated SVD) of <code>X</code></p>
