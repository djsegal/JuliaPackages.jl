<p dir="auto">DataFrameIntervals provides two functions that are handy for computing joins over intervals
of time: <code>interval_join</code> and <code>groupby_interval_join</code>, and a helper function called <code>quantile_windows</code>. See their doc strings for details.</p>
<p dir="auto">Rows match in this join if their time spans overlap. The time spans can be represented as</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://juliapackages.com/p/timespans" rel="nofollow"><code>TimeSpan</code></a> objects</li>
<li><a href="https://juliapackages.com/p/intervals" rel="nofollow"><code>Interval</code></a> objects.</li>
<li><code>NamedTuples</code> with a <code>start</code> and <code>stop</code> field.</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">There are several options to support additional types, such as AlignedSpans. One option is to add interface methods to support automatic conversions to intervals; see e.g. <a href="https://github.com/beacon-biosignals/DataFrameIntervals.jl/pull/13" data-hovercard-type="pull_request" data-hovercard-url="/beacon-biosignals/DataFrameIntervals.jl/pull/13/hovercard">#13</a>. Another option is to manually convert to a supported type; this can provide additional control over how the conversion takes place. For example, one can simply convert to <code>TimeSpan</code>s:</p>

<p dir="auto">Below is a list of related packages and a brief indication of their differences from <code>DataFrameIntervals</code>.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://github.com/xKDR/TSx.jl">TSx</a> various operations on time series data: includes many features DataFrameIntervals does not aim to implement. Does not implement joins over intervals of time.</li>
<li><a href="https://gitlab.com/aplavin/FlexiJoins.jl" rel="nofollow">FlexiJoins</a> generic join operations, including by interval predicates (<code>∈, ⊆, ⊊, ⊋, ⊇, !isdisjoint</code>): the algorithms applied here are more general purpose and are bound by the complexity of more general purpose data structures (e.g. KD-trees). DataFrameIntervals is (currently) bound by a lower complexity class for its specific use case.</li>
<li><a href="https://sl-solution.github.io/InMemoryDatasets.jl/stable/man/joins/#Inequality-kind-joins" rel="nofollow">InMemoryDatasets.jl</a> includes inequality-like joins over intervals of time (where the interval is represented as two columns); this cannot yet achieve the behavior implemented in <code>DataFrameIntervals</code>, where multiple inequalities must be checked to determine overlap.</li>
</ul>
