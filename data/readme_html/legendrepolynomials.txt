<p dir="auto">To compute the Legendre polynomials for a given argument <code>x</code> and a degree <code>l</code>, use <code>Pl(x,l)</code>:</p>

<p dir="auto">To compute the associated Legendre polynomial of degree <code>l,m</code> at the argument <code>x</code>, use <code>Plm(x, l, m)</code>:</p>

<p dir="auto">To compute the n-th derivative of the Legendre polynomial of degree <code>l</code> at the argument <code>x</code>, use <code>dnPl(x, l, n)</code>:</p>
