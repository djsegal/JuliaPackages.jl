<p dir="auto">Decapodes.jl is a framework for developing, composing, and simulating physical systems.</p>
<p dir="auto">Decapodes.jl is the synthesis of Applied Category Theory (ACT) techniques for formalizing and composing physics equations, and Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC) techniques for formalizing differential operators.
<a href="https://algebraicjulia.github.io/CombinatorialSpaces.jl/dev/" rel="nofollow">CombinatorialSpaces.jl</a> hosts tools for discretizing space and defining DEC operators on simplicial complexes, and <a href="https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/DiagrammaticEquations.jl">DiagrammaticEquations.jl</a> hosts tooling for representing the equations as formal ACT diagrams. This repository combines these two packages, compiling diagrams down to simulatable code.</p>
<p dir="auto">By combining the power of ACT and the DEC, we seek to improve the scientific computing workflow. Decapodes simulations are <a href="https://algebraicjulia.github.io/Decapodes.jl/dev/bsh/budyko_sellers_halfar/" rel="nofollow">hierarchically composable</a>, generalize over <a href="https://algebraicjulia.github.io/Decapodes.jl/dev/ice_dynamics/ice_dynamics/" rel="nofollow">any type of manifold</a>, and are <a href="https://www.cise.ufl.edu/~luke.morris/cism.html" rel="nofollow">performant and accurate</a> with a declarative domain specific language (DSL) that is <a href="https://algebraicjulia.github.io/Decapodes.jl/dev/klausmeier/klausmeier/#Model-Representation" rel="nofollow">human-readable</a>.</p>
