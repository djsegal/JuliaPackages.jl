<p dir="auto">Package contains submodules, and many functions are indended to be qualified on call. This lets us use names such as <code>mean()</code> and still have no conflicts with functions in <code>Statistics</code> or <code>StatsBase</code>.</p>

<p dir="auto">All circular statistics operate in a 2π range by default, that corresponds to the natural range of angles. Arbitrary ranges are supported and can be specified as an interval:</p>
