<p dir="auto">Provides <code>TensorField{B,F,N} &lt;: GlobalFiber{LocalTensor{B,F},N}</code> implementation for both a local <code>ProductSpace</code> and general <code>ImmersedTopology</code> specifications on any <code>AbstractFrameBundle</code> expressed with <a href="https://github.com/chakravala/Grassmann.jl">Grassmann.jl</a> algebra.
Many of these modular methods can work on input meshes or product topologies of any dimension, although there are some methods which are specialized.
Building on this, <code>Cartan</code> provides an algebra for any <code>GlobalSection</code> and associated bundles on a manifold, such as general <code>Connection</code> and <code>CovariantDerivative</code> operators in terms of <code>Grassmann</code> elements.
Utility package for differential geometry and tensor calculus intended for <a href="https://github.com/chakravala/Adapode.jl">Adapode.jl</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">The <code>Cartan</code> package is intended to standardize the composition of various methods and functors applied to specialized categories transformed with a unified representation over a product topology, especially having fibers of the <code>Grassmann</code> algebra.
Initial topologies include <code>ProductSpace</code> types and in general the <code>ImmersedTopology</code>.</p>

<p dir="auto">In the <code>Cartan</code> package, a technique is employed where an identity <code>TensorField</code> is constructed from an interval or product manifold, to generate an algebra of sections which can be used to compose parametric maps on manifolds.
Constructing a <code>TensorField</code> can be accomplished in various ways,
there are explicit techniques to construct a <code>TensorField</code> as well as implicit methods.
Additional packages such as <code>Adapode</code> build on the <code>TensorField</code> concept by generating them from differential equations.
Many of these methods can automatically generalize to higher dimensional manifolds and are compatible with discrete differential geometry.</p>
