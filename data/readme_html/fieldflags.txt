<p dir="auto">FieldFlags.jl is a small package for declaring <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bit_field" rel="nofollow">bitfield</a>-like structs, without
having to manually mask out bits. For more information, check out the <a href="https://seelengrab.github.io/FieldFlags.jl/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a>!</p>
