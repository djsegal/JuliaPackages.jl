<p dir="auto"><code>VeriQuEST.jl</code> is a measurement based quantum computing package. To complete the quatnum emulation, the C library <a href="https://github.com/QuEST-Kit/QuEST">QuEST</a>, is used. VeriQuEST's purpose is to emulate noisy verification protocols.</p>
