<p dir="auto">⬇️ <strong>Installation:</strong> use this command in the Julia REPL: <code>using Pkg; Pkg.add("HTMLTables")</code></p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Examples:</strong></p>

<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="using HTMLTables, DataFrames
