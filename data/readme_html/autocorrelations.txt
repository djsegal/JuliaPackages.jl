<p dir="auto">Fast evaluation of autocorrelation functions</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$$f(\tau) = \mathbb{E}\left[X(t)X(t+\tau)\right]$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">for scalar processes <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$X$</math-renderer> or</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$$f(\tau) = \mathbb{E}\left[\mathbf{X}(t)\cdot\mathbf{X}(t+\tau)\right]$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">for vector processes <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$\mathbf{X}$</math-renderer>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Specifically, the <code>acf</code> function evaluates autocorrelation estimates
of the form</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$$\hat{f}_\tau = \dfrac{1}{T-\tau} \sum_{t=1}^{T-\tau} X_t X_{t+\tau},
\qquad \tau \in [0, T-1]$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto">with optional arguments to subtract the mean of the process or to
normalize the resulting autocorrelation function.</p>
<p dir="auto"><code>acf(x; demean=true)</code>:</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$$\hat{f}_\tau = \dfrac{1}{T-\tau} \sum_{t=1}^{T-\tau} (X_t-\bar{X}) (X_{t+\tau}-\bar{X}),
\qquad \tau \in [0, T-1]$$</math-renderer>
<p dir="auto"><code>acf(x; normalize=true)</code>:</p>
<math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="52592b07db2af61b83a6b8fc093051a7">$$\hat{f}_\tau = \dfrac{1}{\hat{f}_0(T-\tau)} \sum_{t=1}^{T-\tau} X_t X_{t+\tau},
\qquad \tau \in [0, T-1]$$</math-renderer>
