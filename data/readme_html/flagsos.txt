<p dir="auto">A julia package for extremal combinatorics based on the flag algebra method and its variants. The package offers various hierarchies to compute lower bounds for problems of the form</p>
<p dir="auto"><math-renderer class="js-display-math" style="display: block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$$
\begin{aligned}
\inf_M\enspace &amp; F(M)\\
\text{s.t. }&amp; G_i(M) \geq 0 \quad\text{ for }i=1,\dots,k,\\
&amp; H_i(M) = 0 \quad\text{ for }i = 1,\dots, \ell,
\end{aligned}
$$</math-renderer></p>
<p dir="auto">where <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$F$</math-renderer>, <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$G_i$</math-renderer>, <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$H_i$</math-renderer> are quantum flags (linear combinations of sub-model density functions), and <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$M$</math-renderer> is a model of fixed size or a converging sequence in a given theory. The constraints can include labels, but then the entire <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="286e0a9e75feabd5153b904406f761a2">$S_n$</math-renderer> orbit needs to be included.</p>
<p dir="auto">Check out the <a href="https://DanielBrosch.github.io/FlagSOS.jl/" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> for details.</p>
