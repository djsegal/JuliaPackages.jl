<p dir="auto">Provide the 8-bits floats (<code>FP8</code>) proposed in <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05433" rel="nofollow">FP8 Formats for Deep Learning</a> (<code>Float8_E4M3FN</code>, <code>Float8_E5M2</code>) and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02915" rel="nofollow">8-bit Numerical Formats For Deep Neural Networks</a> (<code>Float8_E4M3FNUZ</code>, <code>Float8_E5M2FNUZ</code>). Mainly for handling data stored in this format. All floating-point arithmetics are with <code>Float32</code> and convert the output back to <code>FP8</code>.</p>
