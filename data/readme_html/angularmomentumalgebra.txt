<p dir="auto">This library is a thin wrapper around <a href="https://github.com/JuliaAtoms/EnergyExpressions.jl">EnergyExpressions.jl</a> for finding
  energy expressions of atomic configurations. At the moment, it only
  support configurations of spin-orbitals, but in the future, support
  for atomic configuration-state functions is a possibility.</p>
