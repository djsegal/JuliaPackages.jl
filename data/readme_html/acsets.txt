<p dir="auto">Acsets ("attributed C-sets") are a family of data structures generalizing both
graphs and data frames. They are an efficient in-memory implementation of a
category-theoretic formalism for relational databases.</p>
<p dir="auto">This lightweight package provides</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>data structures for acset schemas, acsets, and tabular columns</li>
<li>serialization of acset schemas and acsets</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/Catlab.jl">Catlab.jl</a> extends this package to
offer many more features, beginning with homomorphisms between acsets and
including limits and colimits of acsets, functorial data migration, and
automated homomorphism finding.
<a href="https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/AlgebraicRewriting.jl">AlgebraicRewriting.jl</a>
goes further still to provide declarative rewriting for acsets.</p>

<p dir="auto">Graphs, and their generalization as C-sets, are introduced with minimal
prerequisites in a series of blog posts on the <a href="https://blog.algebraicjulia.org/" rel="nofollow">AlgebraicJulia
blog</a>:</p>
<ol dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://blog.algebraicjulia.org/post/2020/09/cset-graphs-1/" rel="nofollow">Graphs and C-sets I</a>:
What is a graph?</li>
<li><a href="https://blog.algebraicjulia.org/post/2020/09/cset-graphs-2/" rel="nofollow">Graphs and C-sets II</a>:
