<ol dir="auto">
<li><code>Chemical</code>: unstructured chemicals, storing name, formula, and other information; <code>Chemical(name::String, formula::String, info::Vector{Pair{Symbol, Any}})</code>.</li>
<li><code>Ion</code>: charged <code>Chemical</code> with specific adduct or molecule loss; <code>Ion(core::AbstractChemical, adduct::AbstractAdduct)</code>.</li>
<li><code>IonCluster</code>: multiple <code>Ion</code>s with similar m/z; <code>IonCluster(ions::Vector{&lt;: AbstractIon}, abundance::Vector{Float64})</code>.</li>
</ol>

<p dir="auto">User can custumize adduct by <code>PosAdduct</code> and <code>NegAdduct</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">For example, [2M+H]+, and [M-2H-2H2O]2- can be created by <code>PosAdduct(2, "+H", 1)</code>, and <code>NegAdduct(1, "-2H-2H2O", 2)</code> respectively.</p>

<ul dir="auto">
<li><code>chemicalname</code>: name of a chemical (<code>AbstractChemical</code>).</li>
<li><code>chemicalformula</code>: formula of a chemical.</li>
<li><code>ischemicalequal</code>: whether two chemicals equal.</li>
<li><code>ioncore</code>: core of an ion.</li>
<li><code>ionadduct</code>: adduct of an ion.</li>
<li><code>kmer</code>: number of "M" of an adduct or ion.</li>
<li><code>charge</code>: number of charges of an adduct or ion.</li>
<li><code>adductelement</code>: changes of elements of an adduct or ion.</li>
<li><code>adductformula</code>: formula of an adduct.</li>
<li><code>abundantion</code>: most abundant ion of an <code>IonCluster</code>.</li>
<li><code>isionequal</code>: whether two ions equal.</li>
<li><code>isionequal</code>: whether two adducts equal.</li>
<li><code>rt</code>: retention time.</li>
</ul>
