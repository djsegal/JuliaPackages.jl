<p dir="auto">This package implements atmospheric models for the <strong>SatelliteToolbox.jl</strong> ecosystem.
Currently, the following models are available:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Exponential atmospheric model;</li>
<li>Jacchia-Roberts 1971;</li>
<li><a href="http://sol.spacenvironment.net/jb2008/" rel="nofollow">Jacchia-Bowman 2008</a>; and</li>
<li><a href="https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/modelweb/models/nrlmsise00.php" rel="nofollow">NRLMSISE-00</a>.</li>
</ul>
