<p dir="auto"><em>Efficient inverse statistical mechanical algorithms to generate effective potentials or configurations from pair correlation functions or structure factors.</em></p>

<ul dir="auto">
<li>Solves Ornstein-Zernike equations to find approximate potentials for given target <code>g_2(r)</code> and <code>S(k)</code>, supports the following closures:
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Potential of mean force</li>
<li>Mean field approximation</li>
<li>Hypernetted chain</li>
<li>Percus-Yevick</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Torquato-Wang algorithm (Torquato and Wang, PRE 2020) to find optimized parametrized potentials given target <code>g_2(r)</code> and <code>S(k)</code></li>
<li>Iterative Boltzmann Inversion (Soper, Chem. Phys. 1996) to find optimimzed binned potentials given target <code>g_2(r)</code></li>
<li>Reverse Monte-Carlo algorithm (McGreevy, J. Phys. Cond. Matter 2001) to find configuration that match target <code>g_2(r)</code></li>
<li>More to come!</li>
</ul>
