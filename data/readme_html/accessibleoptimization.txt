<p dir="auto">Combining <code>Accessors.jl</code> + <code>Optimization.jl</code> to enable function optimization with arbitrary structs. Vary struct parameters, combinations and transformations of them. Uniform and composable, zero overhead.</p>
<p dir="auto">Defining features of <code>AccessibleOptimization</code>:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>No need to deal with raw vectors of optimized parameters: their values are automatically put into model structs</li>
<li>Can use arbitrary structs as model definitions, no requirements or limitations on their content, types, or methods</li>
<li>Can flexibly specify the list of parameters to optimize, as well as their bounds and constrains - independently of the model struct/object creation</li>
<li>Everything is type-stable and performant</li>
</ul>

<p dir="auto">Suppose you want to fit a model to data. Here, our model is a sum of squared exponentials.</p>
<p dir="auto">First, define your model and a way to evaluate it. Regular Julia code, no special types or functions. This is not <code>Accessors</code>/<code>Optimization</code> specific at all, you may have model definitions already!</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="struct ExpModel{A,B}

<p dir="auto">Then, load <code>AccessibleOptimization</code>, define optimization parameters, and perform optimization:</p>

	(@o log10(_.comps[∗].scale)) =&gt; -1..1,  # component scales: positive-only (using log10 transformation), from 10^-1 to 10^1

<p dir="auto">We use <a href="https://github.com/JuliaObjects/Accessors.jl">Accessors.jl</a> and <a href="https://gitlab.com/aplavin/AccessorsExtra.jl" rel="nofollow">AccessorsExtra.jl</a> optics to specify parameters to vary during optimization. Basic <code>@optic</code> syntax demonstrated in the above example includes property and index access (<code>_.prop</code>, <code>_[1]</code>) and selecting all elements of a collection with <code>_[∗]</code> (type with <code>\ast&lt;tab&gt;</code>). Refer to the <code>Accessors[Extra]</code> documentation for more details.</p>
<p dir="auto">See a Pluto notebook with <a href="https://aplavin.github.io/AccessibleOptimization.jl/examples/notebook.html" rel="nofollow">a walkthrough, more examples and explanations</a>.</p>

<p dir="auto"><code>AccessibleOptimization</code> gains its composability and generality powers (and half its name!) from <code>Accessors</code> and <code>AccessorsExtra</code> packages.</p>
<p dir="auto">The optimization part is directly delegated to <code>Optimization</code>. Other backends are possible, but best to add them as methods to <code>Optimization</code> proper and use from <code>AccessibleOptimization</code>.</p>
