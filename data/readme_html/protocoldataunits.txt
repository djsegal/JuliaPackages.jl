<p dir="auto"><strong>Encoders and decoders for Protocol Data Units (PDUs)</strong></p>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protocol_data_unit" rel="nofollow">PDUs</a> encode information as byte streams that can be transmitted across a network or stored. <code>ProtocolDataUnits.jl</code> simplifies the process of encoding and decoding information as PDUs in a declarative way.</p>

decoded = PDU.decode(bytes, EthernetFrame)

<p dir="auto"><a href="https://github.com/JuliaIO/ProtoBuf.jl">ProtoBuf.jl</a> implements the <a href="https://protobuf.dev" rel="nofollow">Protocol Buffers</a> specification for encoding/decoding data structures into byte streams. While the functionality sounds similar with <code>ProtocolDataUnits.jl</code>, both serve very different needs. Protocol Buffers provide a great way to encode information in a well-defined way but do not provide the flexibility to declare the format of the encoded information. On the other hand, <code>ProtocolDataUnits.jl</code> allows the developer to declare the byte stream format (typically based on networking specifications), and encode/decode the byte streams into structures.</p>
