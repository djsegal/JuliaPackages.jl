<p dir="auto">Simulations of cosmological scalar fields inspired by <a href="https://github.com/auckland-cosmo/PyUltraLight">PyUltraLight</a>.</p>
<p dir="auto">UltraDark uses <a href="https://jipolanco.github.io/PencilFFTs.jl/stable/" rel="nofollow">PencilFFTs</a> to do Fourier transforms in an MPI environment.</p>
