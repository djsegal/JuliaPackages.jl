<p dir="auto"><strong>Hestia.jl</strong> is a <a href="https://julialang.org" rel="nofollow">Julia</a> library to simulate controlled and uncontrolled heat conduction phenomena in multiple dimensions (1D, 2D, 3D). Hestia.jl offers many options to design your heat conduction simulation: geometry, material properties and specification of boundary sides.</p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Geometries</strong>:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>1-dimensional rod of length</li>
<li>2-dimensional plate of lenght L and width W</li>
<li>3-dimensional cuboid of length L, width W, height H</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Material Properties</strong>:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>temperature-independent: <strong>linear</strong> heat conduction</li>
<li>temperature-dependent: <strong>quasi-linear</strong> heat conduction</li>
<li>isotropic</li>
<li>anisotropic</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto"><strong>On boundary sides</strong>:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>emit heat to environment via linear <strong>heat transfer</strong> and nonlinear <strong>heat radiation</strong></li>
<li>supply heat via actuators like heating elements</li>
<li>measure surface temperature</li>
</ul>
