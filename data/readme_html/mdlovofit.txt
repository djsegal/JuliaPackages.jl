<p dir="auto">Julia interface for the <a href="http://m3g.iqm.unicamp.br" rel="nofollow">MDLovoFit</a> package for analyzing the mobility of
structures in MD simulations.</p>

aligned with an RMSD of less than 1 Angstrom. Now we align the
complete trajectory using this fraction as a criterium:</p>
