<p dir="auto">SQLite functions to compress/decompress data in the database.</p>
<p dir="auto">They are created in just a few lines of code, thanks to <code>SQLite.jl</code> UDF interface and to <code>TranscodingStreams.jl</code>.</p>
<p dir="auto">Basic usage:</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="using SQLiteCompress

using DBInterface: execute

<p dir="auto">Performs sanity and consistency check of the compressor-decompressor pair when registering.</p>
<p dir="auto">Tests cover both happy paths and errors, for a range of codecs and for different <code>SQLite.jl</code>-compatible packages.</p>
<p dir="auto">See <a href="https://aplavin.github.io/SQLiteCompress.jl/examples/notebook.html" rel="nofollow">https://aplavin.github.io/SQLiteCompress.jl/examples/notebook.html</a> for benchmarks.</p>
