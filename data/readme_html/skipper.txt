<p dir="auto"><code>skip(predicate, collection)</code>: extension of <code>skipmissing</code> that</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>allows arbitrary predicates, such as <code>ismissing</code>, <code>isnan</code> or <code>x -&gt; isnan(x) || iszero(x)</code></li>
<li>supports setting values</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Alternatively, use <code>keep(predicate, collection)</code> when it is more natural to define what items to keep: for example, <code>keep(&gt;(0), X)</code> instead of <code>skip(x -&gt; isnan(x) || x &lt; 0, X)</code>.</p>
