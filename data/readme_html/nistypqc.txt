<p dir="auto">These are implementations in <a href="https://julialang.org/" rel="nofollow">Julia</a> of various <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-quantum_cryptography" rel="nofollow">post-quantum cryptography</a> (PQC) algorithms that were picked as (candidate) winners in the <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/post-quantum-cryptography/post-quantum-cryptography-standardization" rel="nofollow">Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization Project</a> run by the <a href="https://www.nist.gov/" rel="nofollow">National Institute of Standards and Technology</a> (NIST).
They fall in two categories:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key_encapsulation_mechanism" rel="nofollow">key encapsulation mechanisms</a> (KEM)</li>
<li><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signature" rel="nofollow">digital signature schemes/algorithms</a> (DSA)</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">All implementations in this package strive for simplicity and close resemblance with the specifications.
The focus is not on performance, let alone on security.</p>

<p dir="auto">At the moment, NIST has published draft <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Information_Processing_Standards" rel="nofollow">Federal Information Processing Standards</a> (FIPS) for three of the winners:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>
<p dir="auto">ML-KEM</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>derived from <a href="https://pq-crystals.org/kyber/" rel="nofollow">CRYSTALS-Kyber</a></li>
<li>specified in <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/ipd" rel="nofollow">FIPS 203, Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism Standard</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">ML-DSA</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>derived from <a href="https://pq-crystals.org/dilithium/" rel="nofollow">CRYSTALS-Dilithium</a></li>
<li>specified in <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/ipd" rel="nofollow">FIPS 204, Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Standard</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p dir="auto">SLH-DSA</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>derived from <a href="https://sphincs.org/" rel="nofollow">SPHINCS+</a></li>
<li>specified in <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/ipd" rel="nofollow">FIPS 205, Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Standard</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">There is one more winner with no draft standard available yet:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://falcon-sign.info/" rel="nofollow">Falcon</a> (Fast-Fourier Lattice-based Compact Signatures over NTRU)</li>
</ul>

<p dir="auto">The team of winners might be joined by some of the submissions to <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/post-quantum-cryptography/round-4-submissions" rel="nofollow">Round 4</a> of the standardization project.
Up to now, three of the candidates remain unbroken.
All of them are <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-quantum_cryptography#Code-based_cryptography" rel="nofollow">code-based</a> KEM's.
For the time being, I've included two of them in this package:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://bikesuite.org/" rel="nofollow">BIKE</a> (Bit Flipping Key Encapsulation)</li>
<li><a href="https://classic.mceliece.org/" rel="nofollow">Classic McEliece</a></li>
</ul>
