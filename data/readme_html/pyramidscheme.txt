<p dir="auto">PyramidScheme.jl is a package to easily and efficiently compute pyramids of a given datacube which might be larger than RAM.
It uses DiskArrayEngine.jl as the computational backend.
The pyramids can then be used to interactively explore the data.</p>
<p dir="auto">The long term aim of PyramidScheme.jl is to enable computing based on the layers of the pyramid to enable a more interactive exploration of computations based of pyramided datasets.</p>
