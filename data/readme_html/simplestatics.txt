<p dir="auto">SimpleStatics is a package that assists in the computation and visualization of 2D structure stresses and strains. It's implemented in pure Julia and the content is taken from <a href="https://repository.bakrie.ac.id/10/1/%5BTSI-LIB-131%5D%5BAslam_Kassimali%5D_Matrix_Analysis_of_Structure.pdf" rel="nofollow"><strong>Matrix Analysis of Structures</strong></a> by Kassimali.</p>

<p dir="auto">This package is for people wanting to do quick and <em><strong>simple</strong></em> statics simulations for projects that sit in that awkward middle ground where</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>True FEM software packages are overkill</li>
<li>Working it out on paper is too complicated to do in a few minutes</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Think: <em>I am building a hobby project, but I want to evaluate how much I'll get out of using 3/4" steel tubing over 1/2" tubing, or using a support member <strong>here</strong> rather than <strong>there</strong>.</em></p>
<p dir="auto">It goes without saying (see the <strong>LICENSE</strong>) but <strong>for the love of all that is good, please do not use this for commercial projects or projects where your life or anyone else's depends on the integrity of the structure.</strong></p>
<p dir="auto">Finally, for those of you wanting to use this to check your <em>statics</em> homework, you'll get very close answers, but this program accounts for <em>stress related deformation</em>, which undergrad classes don't typically ask you to do. To get as close as possible, use <code>Materials.PerfectMaterial()</code> for your members.</p>
