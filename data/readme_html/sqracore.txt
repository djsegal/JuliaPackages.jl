<p dir="auto">Using the Square-Root approximation (SQRA) as in [6]:</p>
<p dir="auto">The generetor of the Koopman operator of the diffusion in a potential <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$U$</math-renderer>: <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$dX = -\nabla U dt + \sigma dB_t$</math-renderer>
on a Voronoi tesselation of the state space with centers <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$x_i$</math-renderer> is approximated by the matrix <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$Q$</math-renderer>:</p>
<p dir="auto"><math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$$Q_{ij} = C_{ij} \sqrt\frac{\pi_j}{\pi_i}$$</math-renderer>
for <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$i\neq j$</math-renderer> and <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$Q_{ii} = -\sum_{j\neq i} Q_{ij}$</math-renderer> with <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$$C_{ij} = \beta^{-1}\frac{A_{ij}}{h_{ij} V_i}$$</math-renderer> where <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$A_{ij}$</math-renderer> and <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$V_{ij}$</math-renderer> are the (common boundary) areas and volumes of the cells, <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$h_{ij}$</math-renderer> the distance between the centers of cells <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$i,j$</math-renderer>.</p>
<p dir="auto">It ows its name to the fact that it is proportional to the square-root of the fractions of the stationary distribution <math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$\pi_i$</math-renderer> at the cell centers,
<math-renderer class="js-inline-math" style="display: inline-block" data-static-url="https://github.githubassets.com/static" data-run-id="0cf2a82b89e85337dbfaaac37d5135ac">$$\pi_i = \exp (-\beta U(x_i)).$$</math-renderer></p>
