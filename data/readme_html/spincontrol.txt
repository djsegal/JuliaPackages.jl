<p dir="auto"><strong>SpinControl.jl</strong> is a numerical package written in Julia that makes it easy to simulate the dynamics and decoherence of a spin qubit interacting with environmental spin ensembles.</p>
<p dir="auto">The package provides necessary data types for environmental spin bath, driving pulses, and quantum control protocols, as well as a collection of functions that are widely used in magnetic resonance and quantum control, such as free induction decay (FID) and Rabi driving.
Users can easily use these abstractions to compose their own control sequences such as dynamical decoupling protocols and test their performance on various spin ensembles.</p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>The package is archived as the support material of my master thesis.</strong></p>
<p dir="auto"><strong><a href="http://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:9673e6ba-ff3e-402d-938c-f8d10508cddf" rel="nofollow">Quantum Control of Interacting Spins, Y.N.Zhang, Technische Universiteit Delft</a></strong></p>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://doi.org/10.4121/19766887" rel="nofollow">https://doi.org/10.4121/19766887</a></p>
