<p dir="auto">Empirical Multivariate Cumulative Distribution Function.</p>
<p dir="auto">Main features of <code>MultiCDF.jl</code>:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Provides a way to represent multivariate ECDFs, calculate their values at arbitrary points;</li>
<li>Includes an efficient algorithm to compute ECDF at a grid of points at once;</li>
<li>Supports a variety of point types: <code>Tuple</code>s, <code>NamedTuple</code>s, <code>StaticVector</code>s <em>(doesn't depend on <code>StaticArrays</code> itself)</em>;</li>
<li>Can calculate marginal ECDFs taking a subset of parameters into account;</li>
<li>Computes generalized ECDFs: arbitrary signs (<code>&lt;=</code> / <code>&gt;=</code> / <code>&lt;</code> / <code>&gt;</code>), arbitrary <code>OnlineStats</code> statistics instead of simple counting.</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">See a <a href="https://aplavin.github.io/MultiCDF.jl/test/examples.html" rel="nofollow">Pluto notebook</a> for examples and benchmarks.</p>
