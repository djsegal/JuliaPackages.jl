<p dir="auto">Visualize your data using a few simple building blocks that can be
composed using <code>+</code> and <code>*</code>. AlgebraOfGraphics puts a new algebraic spin
on the grammar of graphics idea known from R’s
<a href="https://ggplot2.tidyverse.org/" rel="nofollow">ggplot2</a> package.</p>
<p dir="auto">Visualizations are powered by
<a href="https://github.com/MakieOrg/Makie.jl">Makie</a> and you have its full
capabilities available to tweak figures produced by AlgebraOfGraphics.</p>
