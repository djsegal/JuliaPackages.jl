<p dir="auto">This is a Julia package for evaluating and transforming Wigner's 𝔇 matrices,
the associated Legendre functions, and spin-weighted spherical harmonics (which
includes the ordinary scalar spherical harmonics).  See <a href="https://moble.github.io/SphericalFunctions.jl/" rel="nofollow">the
documentation</a> for more details.</p>
