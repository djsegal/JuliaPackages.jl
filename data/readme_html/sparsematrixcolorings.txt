<p dir="auto">The algorithms implemented in this package are taken from the following articles:</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li><a href="https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0036144504444711" rel="nofollow"><em>What Color Is Your Jacobian? Graph Coloring for Computing Derivatives</em></a>, Gebremedhin et al. (2005)</li>
<li><a href="https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/050639879" rel="nofollow"><em>New Acyclic and Star Coloring Algorithms with Application to Computing Hessians</em></a>, Gebremedhin et al. (2007)</li>
<li><a href="https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/ijoc.1080.0286" rel="nofollow"><em>Efficient Computation of Sparse Hessians Using Coloring and Automatic Differentiation</em></a>, Gebremedhin et al. (2009)</li>
<li><a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2513109.2513110" rel="nofollow"><em>ColPack: Software for graph coloring and related problems in scientific computing</em></a>, Gebremedhin et al. (2013)</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">Some parts of the articles (like definitions) are thus copied verbatim in the documentation.</p>
