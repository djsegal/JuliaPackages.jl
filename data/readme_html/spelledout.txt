<p dir="auto">This is a minimal package for a pure Julia implementation of converting numbers in their Arabic form to numbers in their Anglo-Saxan form.  The process of <strong>spelling out</strong> a number has also been referred to as <strong>set full out</strong>, to write out in <strong>long hand</strong>, and — as a great mind once suggested — to <strong>transnumerate</strong>.</p>
