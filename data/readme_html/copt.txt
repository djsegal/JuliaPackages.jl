<p dir="auto"><a href="https://github.com/COPT-Public/COPT.jl">COPT.jl</a> is a wrapper for the
<a href="https://www.shanshu.ai/copt" rel="nofollow">COPT (Cardinal Optimizer)</a>, a mathematical
optimization solver for large-scale optimization problems.</p>
<p dir="auto">COPT includes high-performance solvers for LP, MIP, SOCP, convex QP/QCP and SDP.</p>

<p dir="auto">Without a license, you can solve small models for non-commercial purpose. We
strongly recommend that you apply for a license by following the link above.</p>
