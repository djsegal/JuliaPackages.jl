<p dir="auto">I have not tested on every environment. To add compatibility for an observation type that I don't implement <code>convert_obs</code> similar to the ndarray function:</p>
<div class="highlight highlight-source-julia notranslate position-relative overflow-auto" dir="auto" data-snippet-clipboard-copy-content="convert_obs(::Val{:ndarray}, pyobs::Py) = pyconvert(Vector, pyobs)"><pre><span class="pl-en">convert_obs</span>(<span class="pl-k">::</span><span class="pl-c1">Val{:ndarray}</span>, pyobs<span class="pl-k">::</span><span class="pl-c1">Py</span>) <span class="pl-k">=</span> <span class="pl-c1">pyconvert</span>(Vector, pyobs)</pre></div>
<p dir="auto">where <code>:ndarray</code> comes from the call of <code>convert_obs</code> in the GymnasiumEnv wrapper class:</p>
