<p dir="auto">A package providing various polynomial types (assuming different
polynomial bases) beyond the standard basis polynomials in the
<a href="https://github.com/JuliaMath/Polynomials.jl"><code>Polynomials.jl</code></a> package. This package includes many classic orthogonal
polynomials, the Lagrange and Newton interpolating polynomials, and the
Bernstein polynomials.</p>
