<p dir="auto"><code>MultiAgentPathFinding.jl</code> contains some heuristic algorithms (cooperative A* and local search) described in the PhD thesis</p>
<blockquote>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://pastel.hal.science/tel-04053322" rel="nofollow"><em>Machine learning and combinatorial optimization algorithms, with applications to railway planning</em></a>, Dalle (2022)</p>
</blockquote>
<p dir="auto">It also contains a parser for the set of benchmark instances introduced by</p>
<blockquote>
<p dir="auto"><a href="https://www.aaai.org/ocs/index.php/SOCS/SOCS19/paper/view/18341" rel="nofollow"><em>Multi-Agent Pathfinding: Definitions, Variants, and Benchmarks</em></a>, Stern et al. (2019)</p>
</blockquote>
