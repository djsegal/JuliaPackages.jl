<p dir="auto">Building and training a model according to the above <code>config</code> is done with <a href="https://evovest.github.io/NeuroTreeModels.jl/dev/API#NeuroTreeModels.fit" rel="nofollow">NeuroTreeModels.fit</a>.
See the docs for additional features, notably early stopping support through the tracking of an evaluation metric.</p>
