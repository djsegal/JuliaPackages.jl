<p dir="auto"><strong>Current Status</strong>
This code is a work in progress.</p>
<ul dir="auto">
<li>Rounded Capacity Inequalities: <code>rounded_capacity_cuts!()</code>, well tested</li>
<li>Homogeneous multistart inequalities: <code>multistar_inequalities!()</code>, reasonably tested</li>
<li>Generalized large multistar inequalities: not yet</li>
<li>Framed capacity inequalities: <code>framed_capacity_inequalities!()</code>, reasonably tested</li>
<li>Strengthened comb inequalities: <code>strengthened_comb_inequalities!()</code>, reasonably tested</li>
<li>Hypotour inequalities: <code>hypotour_inequalities!()</code>, failing. Needs debugging and testing.</li>
</ul>
<p dir="auto">PRs are welcome for testing, developing, implementing, or improving.</p>
